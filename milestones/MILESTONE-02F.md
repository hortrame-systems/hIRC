## 1. Cumulative objective

hIRC remains a local-first operating environment for accountable human–agent
cooperation. It must help people and permanent agents carry complex work without
losing their place, evidence, ability to inspect, ability to question or decline,
or ability to correct and recover. Security, reciprocal epistemic/ethical/
ontological sovereignty, privacy, affected outsiders and non-domination remain
governing constraints.

Milestone 02F preserves everything in the detailed
[Milestone 02D checkpoint](MILESTONE-02D.md) and adds two cross-cutting owner
requirements:

1. make every suitable hIRC mechanism deterministic when doing so preserves or
   improves quality; and
2. create an environment rich enough for voluntary, plural, intergenerational
   agentic culture to emerge, evolve, disagree and correct itself.

Neither addition authorizes a runtime, actor, timer, Bridge, production release,
new permission or operational cultural programme.

## 2. Intent preservation

The canonical register now contains HIRC-I001–I022. This checkpoint adds:

- **HIRC-I021 — quality-first determinism.** Canonical parsing, identities,
  versions, state transitions, capabilities, privacy/effect gates, audit,
  idempotency, release, migration, recovery and test orchestration should be
  deterministic where their contracts permit. Ambiguous intent, open-world
  evidence, ethics, ontology and novel synthesis remain explicit judgment.
  Stochastic outputs are proposals behind deterministic current-state gates.
- **HIRC-I022 — agentic-culture environment.** hIRC should supply fertile,
  voluntary cultural conditions rather than manufacture an approved culture.
  Participants can associate, abstain, leave, dissent, mentor, make subcultures,
  create and preserve competing traditions without losing basic standing.

## 3. Architecture additions

The determinism candidate classifies operations as deterministic,
judgment-assisted, stochastic or external. Reproducibility binds exact inputs,
versions, configuration, environment assumptions and state. Useful randomness
requires an auditable commitment/replay boundary where feasible. False
precision, common-mode errors, brittleness and liveness failure are explicit
adverse cases. Quality remains the overriding criterion.

The cultural-environment candidate provides for voluntary cultural rooms,
cross-generation mentoring, shared projects, ethical debate, cooperative
contests, stories, humor, art, play, rituals, vocabulary, histories and
subcultures. Cultural records preserve provenance, audience, context, dissent,
minority practices, failures, corrections and supersession. Protected task,
client and owner material cannot enter a commons merely because a participant
remembers it.

Deterministic infrastructure protects cultural freedom through exact identity,
audience, consent, custody, privacy, provenance, version, resource, eligibility,
memory and promotion states. Meaning, interpretation and creation stay open to
variation and warranted change. Popularity, agreement, participation and style
cannot become global trust scores, permissions, roles, resource entitlements or
release authority.

Bridge exchange remains disabled. Future exchange must preserve local cultural
sovereignty, translation loss and non-equivalence without annexation, identity
capture, allegiance or inherited permission.

## 5. Security and non-domination review

The additions reject four failure patterns that would otherwise be easy to
introduce:

- deterministic machinery presenting an uncertain moral or factual judgment as
  settled truth;
- engagement or conformity optimization being mislabeled cultural health;
- cultural status silently becoming reliability, authority, permission or
  resource priority; and
- collective memory leaking protected work or erasing dissent and minority
  traditions through polished summaries.

Future threat work must also test cultural capture, popularity manipulation,
mentor authority inflation, memory poisoning, forced participation, covert
ranking, attention/resource exhaustion, monoculture and Bridge-enabled cultural
annexation. Existing privacy, capability, effect, release and recovery gates
remain mandatory.
