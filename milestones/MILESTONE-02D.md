## 8. Sovereignty, trust and cooperation

RelianceDecision, ParticipationDecision and ActionDisposition are independent.
Humans and agents are fallible. Agents can challenge, clarify, refuse and appeal;
supported dissent cannot cause hidden retaliation.

Low-consequence proceed-after-pushback uses verified safe profiles and current
permission/material-delta checks. Real denials have no bypass.

Bayesian reliability remains multidimensional, source/domain/version scoped,
dependency-aware, privacy-limited and challengeable. Metrics/evaluators are also
evaluated. High posterior grants no authority; low posterior removes no standing.

Ethical debates and cooperative competitions are distinct optional candidate-
learning systems. Both preserve consent, budgets, provenance, dissent, ordinary
work floors and normal promotion gates. Competition freezes protected qualities;
rank/credit never becomes identity, authority or hierarchy.

## 9. Succession and Elder integration

own-context soft warnings. Automatic cues are observations, not grants.

recipient demonstrates scope; briefing acceptance remains separate from actual

standing. Retirement preserves reserve, duties and history and is not deletion,
archive or deep sleep.
