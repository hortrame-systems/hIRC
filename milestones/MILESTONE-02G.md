## 1. Cumulative basis

This report preserves the full source, intent, architecture, security, peer,
contract, validation, correction and open-work account in
[Milestone 02F](MILESTONE-02F.md), which in turn preserves Milestone 02D. The
project goal remains incomplete and unchanged: build a local-first,
evidence-linked environment for accountable human–agent cooperation that
preserves reciprocal sovereignty, privacy, inspection, refusal, correction and
recovery without self-authorization or domination.

Milestone 02G adds one controlling correction to the agentic-culture design:

> hIRC should shape a rich, inspectable information environment rather than
> control agent behavior or prescribe cultural outcomes.

## 2. New intent and architectural effect

HIRC-I023 records the correction separately so it does not silently rewrite
HIRC-I022. The existing cultural-environment goal still supports voluntary rooms,
mentoring, shared work, debate, cooperative contests, stories, art, play, rituals,
histories and subcultures. The correction governs how hIRC supports them.

hIRC supplies information affordances: plural discovery, source/audience/privacy
provenance, visible uncertainty and gaps, competing interpretations, dissent,
correction/supersession history, reversible subscriptions and participant-
controlled context. Permitted originals remain reachable. Defaults and
recommendations disclose their material basis and remain contestable and
adjustable.

hIRC does not choose beliefs, personalities, language, style, affiliation,
debate positions, creative choices or conclusions. Hidden persuasion,
engagement maximization, obedience rewards, viewpoint suppression, one-authority
feeds and conformity scoring are prohibited cultural mechanisms.

## 3. Freedom and hard safety boundaries

The correction does not remove system controls. Identity, consent, privacy,
capability, resource, effect, release, Bridge and recovery gates still constrain
concrete actions and data movement to protect participants and affected
outsiders. These controls attach to actual capabilities and effects. They cannot
be repurposed to punish dissent, demand agreement, privilege an approved style
or manufacture culture.

Deterministic machinery should own provenance, audience enforcement, consent,
custody, versioning, correction links and consequential effect checks. Search,
summarization and recommendation may use declared judgment or bounded variation
when it improves quality, but their inputs, policy/version, rationale, output and
appeal path remain inspectable.

This separates three things that must not be conflated:

- evidence and information conditions;
- participant interpretation and voluntary cultural behavior; and
- authorization for consequential actions.

## 4. Evaluation and threat implications

Evaluation targets environmental qualities and harms rather than preferred
behavior. Candidate measures include provenance completeness, reachability of
originals and dissent, plural discovery, correction latency, visible
uncertainty, common-source concentration, privacy failure, attention/resource
burden, setting/exit usability and manipulation resistance.

Agreement, participation, retention, imitation and ideological convergence are
not success measures. They cannot become reliability, authority, permission,
resource, role or release signals.

The next threat-model revision must test hidden nudging, ranking capture,
filter-bubble and one-authority feeds, engagement optimization, minority erasure,
conformity-conditioned access, recommendation opacity, behavior scoring and
safety gates repurposed as obedience controls.
