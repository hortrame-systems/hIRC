### UIA-H05 — individual refusal and dispute scope cannot silently become collective authority

Support the addition's rejection of majority-issued global shutdown. A concern, supported factual finding, normative assessment, authority decision, participation choice and observed effect are distinct records. A refusal preserves its reason and relevant obligations; it does not by itself establish wrongdoing or authorize other agents' withdrawal.

Expose a concise effect account to the operator and affected participants while retaining legitimately private deliberation. The account identifies affected work, what remains safe, current capacity, deciding party, alternatives and next review. Avoid one alert per repeated accusation. Clearly distinguish a broker prohibition from an individual decision not to participate. Transparent warranted reassignment may be possible; covertly replacing a participant to defeat its protected refusal is not a sufficient solution. Minority disagreement is retained, and a representative binds only its actual consenting mandate.

Acceptance includes unsupported allegations, credible contrary evidence, a valid individual refusal, willing alternative participants, expired mandates, partial withdrawal, a crash during negotiation and a safety controller stabilizing an external device. No global kill, forced return, new authority or replay follows from a vote or a restart. Host/provider confidentiality limits remain explicit.

### UIA-H06 — debated learning is optional admitted work; the commitments remain architectural

The new HIRC-I008/I009 requirement combines architectural commitments with a default debate option for eligible non-busy permanent pairs, progressively complex discussions, collective debate memory and actual choice of domain. Those are different responsibilities. The default option cannot turn stale presence into true idleness, override reservations/queues/refusal, wake paused agents or consume unlimited idle capacity.

Proposed contract: an eligibility event selects from current willing and suitably educated permanent participants inside a bounded resource/attention envelope; pseudorandom pairing records its method/seed or equivalent reproducible choice without treating randomness as fairness proof. The pair can choose among the five commitments, English3.0, Grand Plan and other actually admitted domains. A disagreement, unavailable exact source or inaccessible collective memory yields negotiation, defer or decline rather than a forced topic.

Before the debate, verify each participant's own baseline and whole authorized collective-memory coverage plus attributable deltas. If the required memory exceeds one context, cover it in resumable jobs before debate; do not substitute a retrieval sample or certify comprehension from EOF. Collective scope does not declassify private team records. Preserve source identity, preceding conclusions, corrections, unresolved questions, dissent and the question that justifies increased complexity.

Complexity grows only for a meaningful new challenge; repetition and debate count are not competence. Shared models/sources and prior debate exposure limit independence. Candidate lessons remain candidates. Norm changes use their legitimate governance lane; claims of empirically improved machinery require the frozen quality/comparison/promotion gates. Stable operation and a declined debate remain valid. This review starts no debate, actor or timer and resumes no scheduled constitutional routine.

Acceptance: reserved/unknown/busy participants excluded; willing pairs choose different domains; unavailable memory/source blocks only its dependent discussion; seeded pairing can be inspected for starvation; ordinary work preempts optional debate at a safe boundary; corruption/restart preserves lesson lineage without replaying a discussion as accepted policy; a fast lesson losing refusal/evidence quality is not promoted.
