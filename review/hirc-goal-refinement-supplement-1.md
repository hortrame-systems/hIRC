# hIRC goal refinement supplement 1 — governed evolution

**Artifact ID:** HIRC-GOAL-CANDIDATE-001-S1  
**Owner instruction:** HIRC-I017  
**Status:** active method for the provisional goal; not authority to expand scope
or activate a system feature

The owner permits the hIRC goal to be refined as work proceeds. The mission in
HIRC-GOAL-CANDIDATE-001 is therefore the active **provisional** north star, not
immutable wording and not a final acceptance decision.

Use two change classes:

1. **Clarifying refinement:** improves precision without changing participant
   rights, scope, trade-offs, security/privacy, licensing, authority or accepted
   outcomes. Record an attributable textual/semantic diff and update the working
   draft.
2. **Consequence-changing refinement:** adds/removes an objective, changes a
   priority or trade-off, or affects parties, authority, risk, scope, phases,
   licensing or acceptance. Create a `GoalChange` with evidence, alternatives,
   dissent and an impact graph across requirements, threats, contracts, tests,
   metrics, roadmap and migration. It receives peer/owner visibility and cannot
   authorize itself.

Every `GoalVersion` preserves exact predecessor wording, reason, retained and
changed meaning, source/owner instruction, active interval and compatibility for
work already in progress. A milestone may propose a refinement or a justified
`NO_CHANGE`.

Goal evolution cannot cancel an explicit requirement by omission, invent actor
or effect authority, resume paused work, weaken privacy/security/non-domination,
activate licensing or Bridge, or redefine a failing metric as success. Metrics
serve the goal and stay plural; the goal does not become engagement, obedience,
scale, agreement or score maximization.
