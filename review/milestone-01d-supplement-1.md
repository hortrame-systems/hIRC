# Milestone 01D supplement 1 — recursive Bayesian reliance

**Artifact ID:** HIRC-M01D-S1  
**Status:** bounded architecture intake; peer consensus remains open

The owner refined the sovereignty/trust requirement: trust evolves recursively
through Bayesian evaluation of interactions and objective metrics for everyone
and everything, and no authority is blindly accepted.

The refinement rules out a static trust label. It also exposes a new domination
risk: metrics, priors and evaluators can encode power while appearing objective.
The architecture therefore records multidimensional scoped posteriors, governs
metric validity/versioning, models dependence and drift, evaluates evaluators at
bounded depth, and keeps authority/consent/effect gates independent.

Next peer challenge focuses on usability without false precision, metric and
prior governance, privacy, recovery from bad posteriors, authority verification
cost and resistance to Goodhart/evaluator capture. No trust service, surveillance
collection or authority change occurred.
