## Holistic assessment

The plan's strongest contribution is a nonviolent separation of individual
refusal, deterministic action denial, evidence review, voluntary coordination,
bounded representation, safe continuity, correction and restoration. It also
places reciprocal limits on the author/licensor and preserves criticism, exit,
portable records and nonretaliation. Those are compatible with the hIRC
foundation.

The main failure risk is authority laundering through ethical language. An
accusation, corporate form, model consensus, private council, source owner,
payment status or claim of domination cannot become truth, jurisdiction or
technical capability by naming itself. The product must preserve factual,
normative, authority, participation, action, legal and performance states
separately.

The licensing layer creates a second risk: a live self-improving foundation
cannot silently rewrite an accepted historical licence. Behavioural semantics
may evolve through the foundation's governed path; legal grants, price schedules
and entitlements remain explicit, separately versioned and bound to their own
formation, notice, acceptance and remedy rules.

The Bridge remains the largest hostile ingress. Licensing, release, recovery
and foundation roots still have larger possible internal blast radii. Licence
signing therefore receives a separate narrow key and no authority over software,
identity, Bridge, data, audit or recovery.

## Refined lens

> The system may embody its ethical and epistemic foundation without jargon,
> but an obligation that changes a person's legal or economic rights must remain
> explicit, version-bound and contestable. Refusal protects the refusing
> participant; it does not create jurisdiction over others. Coordination can
> organize consent and evidence, never manufacture truth or power.
