# Milestone 02 supplement 1 — provisional goal evolution

**Artifact ID:** HIRC-M02-S1  
**Status:** bounded method update; peer consensus remains open

The owner authorized goal refinement during the work. The current mission becomes
the active provisional north star. It can improve as evidence exposes omissions
or conflicts, but it is not a self-grant.

The refined milestone lens now asks two questions: does a feature serve the
current goal, and does the goal itself still represent the affected parties,
evidence, risks and owner intent? A material change must expose its complete
impact and cannot optimize one metric by sacrificing a protected anti-goal.

The Master Plan working draft now includes versioned GoalVersion/GoalChange
semantics. Requirements and consensus matrices must still ingest U166-U168 and
GRM-001-GRM-005 before plan closure.
