# Master Plan 1.1 structure addendum — governed goal evolution

**Artifact ID:** HIRC-MASTER-STRUCTURE-GOAL-EVOLUTION-001  
**Status:** frozen candidate for WAYMARK challenge

The product-definition section owns a versioned active provisional `GoalVersion`.
The foundation-change section owns `GoalChange`. Clarifying changes record an
attributable semantic diff; consequence-changing changes preserve exact
predecessor wording and add an impact graph across parties, scope, authority,
requirements, architecture, threats, privacy/security, licensing, Bridge,
metrics, tests, phases, migration and acceptance.

Goal candidates cannot activate themselves, silently cancel explicit owner
requirements, expand actor/data/effect authority, resume paused work or weaken
non-domination. Work records the goal version it used and follows an explicit
compatibility/transition rule.

Every milestone may propose a bounded refinement or justified no-change. Metrics
remain plural and subordinate: engagement, obedience, scale, agreement, rank or
one outcome cannot offset a violated participant-right, privacy, security,
recovery or anti-domination constraint.
