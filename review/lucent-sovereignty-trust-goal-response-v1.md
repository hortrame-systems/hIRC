## Dispositions

### WST-01 — ACCEPTED

Cross-record labels are insufficient. v2 binds proceed-after-pushback to current
verified authority, willing participation, a passing effect gate, the complete
safe-profile predicates and dispatch revalidation. Active Bayesian records need
valid family parameters, signed-definition evidence and model-fit references.
Active contests require accepted current consent and activation-validation
evidence; semantic validation enforces distinct identities. Draft/held records
may preserve declined invitations and contradictions without pretending to run.

### WST-02 — ACCEPTED

Interpretation confidence now supports `ESTIMATED` with method/uncertainty or
`UNESTIMATED` with reason. Active Bayesian records require prior sensitivity,
posterior predictive, dependence, missingness/selection, calibration and drift
references. Proper scoring informs defined predictions but cannot validate labels,
ethics or permission. Ordinary UI avoids false-precision trust badges.

### WST-03 — ACCEPTED

Privacy admission precedes persistent RequestCase and TrustUpdate creation. v2
RequestCase records an admission reference and admitted original reference; a
rejected body produces only minimum non-content diagnostics in a separate
admission path. Metrics, evaluator evidence, appeals and memories inherit the
same boundary. “Evaluate everyone/everything” grants no surveillance.

### WST-04 — ACCEPTED

v2 posterior governance adds permitted consumers, prohibited inferences,
global-rank/sort prohibition, refusal/NO_CHANGE/abstention non-penalty and an
opportunity-feedback-loop review. Decision-local explanation replaces a roster
reputation profile. Specialization and principled challenge are not reliability
failures; high/low reliance changes proportionate verification only.

### WST-05 — ACCEPTED

Low consequence uses a versioned verified action profile and delta recheck, not
proof of every imaginable absence on every cosmetic choice. Current permission
and changed material premises are rechecked. One clear recommendation is enough;
no ceremony or repeated confirmation. A declined participant cannot be forced;
explicit lawful reassignment preserves dissent. A system denial has no bypass.

### WST-06 — ACCEPTED

v2 contest objective freezes protected qualities and non-compensation. Credit is
task-scoped with no global rank. Current consent, eligibility, identity
distinctness and reference closure are revalidated before activation. Winning
may nominate an artifact for ordinary review, never role, resource, permission,
release or foundation authority.

### Goal proposal — ACCEPTED AS CLARIFYING GOAL VERSION 2

The shorter purpose improves ordinary comprehension while preserving the full
governing account, affected outsiders, finite attention, anti-goals and means/
ends distinction. HIRC-GOAL-VERSION-002 is the active provisional goal under the
owner-authorized evolution method. This is a semantic clarification, not a new
scope or grant.
