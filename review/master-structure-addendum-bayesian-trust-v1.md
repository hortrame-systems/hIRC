# Master Plan 1.1 structure addendum — recursive Bayesian reliance

**Artifact ID:** HIRC-MASTER-STRUCTURE-BAYESIAN-TRUST-001  
**Status:** frozen candidate for WAYMARK challenge  
**Applies to:** HIRC-MASTER-STRUCTURE-SOVEREIGNTY-COMPETITION-001

Extend the trust/provenance section with multidimensional, action-specific
Bayesian reliance for every human, agent, model, source, tool, sensor, metric,
evaluator, process, provider and connected system. No class receives an
infallibility exemption, including owners and hIRC's own evaluators.

Every posterior binds subject, domain/action dimension, context, model and metric
version, prior provenance, evidence window, dependence clusters, effective
sample size, uncertainty, calibration, drift and expiry. Unknown or missing
outcomes never become zero, success or failure. There is no universal human-worth
or obedience score.

Add signed/versioned metric and evaluator registries. Metrics declare construct,
unit, collection/sampling, noise, privacy, missingness/selection/dependence,
gaming and distribution-shift limits. Recursive evaluation covers primary
outcomes, metric validity, evaluator calibration/conflicts and trust-model
assumptions at a bounded depth; unresolved regress remains held.

Keep authority orthogonal. Each consequential authority claim independently
verifies principal, delegation, action/target/data/time/purpose scope, expiry,
revocation, conflicts, consent and legitimacy. High reliability grants no
authority or gate bypass; valid authority supplies no truth exemption. Low
reliability removes no equal standing or access required for correction/appeal.

Add privacy/non-domination controls against protected traits and proxies,
surveillance expansion, feedback-loop discrimination and posterior-to-permission
leakage. Explain decisive evidence, correlation, domain/version limits, metric
changes and unknown observations in plain language at the appropriate audience.

Delivery sequence: authority verification and no-infallibility/no-permission-
leak invariants in P0; posterior/metric/evaluator records and privacy-safe update
pipeline in P1-T; recursive calibration, drift, missingness, dependence and
sensitivity evaluation in P2-T; bounded use in competition/debate only after
adverse evidence and interaction review.
