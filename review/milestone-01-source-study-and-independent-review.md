## Holistic assessment of the prior work

### Evidence

The product thesis, attention architecture, provenance distinctions, recovery
orientation, local autonomy, and several default-deny boundaries are strong.
The Bridge and system-security sections remain design sketches. The transfer
does not include the fingerprinted prototype source objects needed to rerun its
reported 73 tests. The governing-source bindings are stale. Temporary-subagent
unbanning conflicts with current owner policy.

### Inference

The largest shared failure mode is authority laundering through a convenience
layer. A model summary, prompt stack, role badge, plugin, provider adapter,
attention governor, migration helper, bridge translation, or recovery path can
quietly acquire decision power unless every effect returns to one deterministic
identity, data, authority, and consequence boundary.

The Bridge is not merely another adapter. It combines an adversarial network,
foreign identity, foreign ontology, foreign data classification, uncertain
delivery, cryptography, translation by models, and local tool authority. Its
security must be a separate gated program.

### Normative and relational effects

The design should increase the capacity of people, agents, and connected
systems to inspect evidence, refuse, correct, recover, and remain locally
self-governing. Cooperation must not require memory merger, hidden dependency,
ontology capture, automatic membership, or surrender of private context. The
same scrutiny applies when hIRC itself would benefit from a broader bridge or
more data.

### Authority

The owner authorized source study, adversarial review with UI-A, master-plan
revision, and LUCENT's security-specialist function. No live provider call,
credential use, new actor, bridge activation, public disclosure, production
release, or weakening of the temporary-subagent ban was authorized.

## Refined lens

The next milestone uses this refinement:

> A governing foundation is real only when it remains in the causal path from
> source and evidence to interpretation, authority, action, correction, and
> recovery. Every layer that saves attention or translates complexity must be
> tested as a possible authority-laundering and dependency-creation boundary.

This refinement does not demand more visible terminology or more review
ceremony. It demands fewer bypasses, plain explanations at consequence
boundaries, source-linked inspection, preserved dissent, and mechanical
enforcement where judgment is no longer needed.
