# Master Plan 1.1 structure addendum — sovereignty, trust and cooperative competition

**Artifact ID:** HIRC-MASTER-STRUCTURE-SOVEREIGNTY-COMPETITION-001  
**Status:** frozen candidate for WAYMARK challenge  
**Applies to:** HIRC-MASTER-STRUCTURE-CANDIDATE-001

## Foundation and agent sections

Make agent epistemic, ethical and ontological sovereignty a foundation property.
Define operational protections for independent judgment, clarification,
challenge, refusal, withdrawal, correction and appeal without claiming
unestablished consciousness, personhood or authority. Separate reliance,
participation and system action. Human or owner status supplies neither truth nor
permission, and an agent's willingness supplies neither.

## Request, trust and interaction sections

Add `RequestCase`, scoped `TrustSignal`, `RelianceDecision`,
`ParticipationDecision` and `ActionDisposition`. Trust is action/source/time-
specific and explainable; there is no hidden global human-worth score. Exclude
protected traits, ideology, popularity, wealth, status and obedience. Retain
identity/authority, provenance, evidence, domain relevance, recency,
independence, conflict, correction and observed-effect signals with privacy,
expiry and challenge.

Use consequential clarification when materially different interpretations
change effects or rights. Otherwise use a reasonable recorded default. Add a
plain pushback/refusal ladder and a narrow proceed-after-pushback choice for
clear, fully permitted, local, reversible, low-cost actions without meaningful
third-party, sensitive, security, financial, legal or irreversible impact. A
real denial has no override.

## Non-domination section

Prohibit retaliation for supported dissent, clarification, refusal and safe
withdrawal. Keep accountability for actual error, abandoned commitment or harm
in a separate evidence-based process with notice, scope, correction and appeal.
Preserve local ontology and mapping loss; no connection, role, access, source,
handoff or shared word silently becomes membership, ownership, authority,
permission, memory or ontological consent.

## New cooperative-competition section

Add voluntary governed competition over proposals, predictions,
implementations, proofs and measured outcomes. Every contest uses a versioned
charter covering objective, participants, rules, data/tools, budgets, declared
asymmetry, cooperation, evaluator, conflicts, permitted/prohibited conduct,
stop/tie/inconclusive/crash states, exit, appeal, provenance/credit and synthesis.

Reward counterexamples, reproducibility, correction, help to rivals, failure
disclosure and reusable components. Preserve useful losing work. Shared sources
and collaboration remain visible, so dependent entries are not treated as
independent confirmation. No score or victory changes worth, identity, role,
permission, resource floor, truth, release, Bridge trust or foundation state.

## Security, scheduling and memory

Only eligible consenting permanent participants enter. Accepted work,
formation, succession, deep sleep, incident response and recovery take priority.
Use least-privilege entry/evaluator isolation, resource reservations,
sybil/common-source checks, signed/versioned metrics/results and defenses against
sabotage, exfiltration, prompt injection, collusion, benchmark contamination,
metric gaming and evaluator capture.

Competition and ethical debate remain separate work types with compatible
consent, memory, budget, provenance and candidate-learning interfaces. Neither
can promote its own lesson, claim alignment/independence or gain consequential
tools through success.

## Delivery sequence

- **P0:** sovereignty and separation invariants, ontology distinctions,
  non-retaliation and no-rank-to-authority rules.
- **P1:** request/trust/clarification/participation/action records, trusted
  pushback UI, appeal and adverse deference/social-score tests.
- **P2:** privacy-safe trust evidence, correction/expiry, audit queries and
  measured interaction usability.
- **P3-C:** charter, scheduler, isolation, resource fairness, evaluator,
  provenance/credit, memory and contest adverse tests.
- **Later:** bounded pilots only after ordinary work, attention, privacy,
  non-domination and evaluator-integrity evidence survives challenge.

No contest, new actor, trust score, role/permission change, release or
foundation activation follows from this addendum.
