# Master Plan 1.1 structure addendum — idle ethical debates and collective memory

**Artifact ID:** HIRC-MASTER-STRUCTURE-DEBATE-001  
**Status:** frozen candidate for UI-A challenge  
**Applies to:** HIRC-MASTER-STRUCTURE-CANDIDATE-001

## Foundation section

State that alignment with the five commitments is part of the architecture and
whole-source coverage. Keep participant-selected debate subjects separate: the
five commitments, Epistemethics and the Grand Plan are required `DebateDomain`
choices, each bound to its exact current source and memory. The full foundation
governs how a debate operates without forcing every debate to have the same
subject.

## New collective-learning section

Add **Idle ethical deliberation and collective debate memory** after the
foundation/memory foundations and before large-scale agent organization. It
owns:

- default-permitted but controllable idle debate policy;
- permanent-agent eligibility, consent, opt-out and no-work-preemption;
- constrained-random fair pair selection and atomic reservation;
- participant domain choice and clean no-shared-domain exit;
- exact source and whole/delta collective-memory study;
- per-domain memory partitions, counterexamples and unresolved frontiers;
- multidimensional complexity progression from actual surviving questions;
- bounded debate protocol, no-effect sandbox and recovery;
- synthesis, dissent, candidate lessons and foundation-promotion separation;
- resource/attention backpressure and quiet operation; and
- claims/nonclaims and adversarial acceptance evidence.

## Agent, scheduling and communication sections

Presence remains distinct from idle eligibility. Pairing uses one exact scheduler
transaction, immutable identities, no temporary agents and no cross-team data.
Debate rooms use bounded speaker and termination contracts. Either agent can
decline a pair, domain, topic or turn without penalty. No debate reassigns
ordinary work, creates a role/experience prefix, or changes an agent's
availability outside its reservation.

## Memory and foundation-change sections

The collective debate memory is a governed knowledge domain with permitted
original turns, source/model/runtime provenance, claims, evidence,
counterexamples, failures, dissent, corrections, syntheses and candidate
lessons. Summaries and indexes remain derivative. A valid prior whole-memory
checkpoint plus complete immutable delta can satisfy later study; otherwise the
debate stays queued. Coverage is not comprehension.

Candidate lessons can guide later topics and training proposals. They cannot
change the foundation, licence, permissions, safety, security, Bridge, release
or operational behavior without the normal independent review and
`FoundationChange` path.

## Delivery sequence

- **P0/P1:** foundation source/coverage and non-effectful learning-policy
  invariants; no debate execution.
- **P2-D:** debate-memory schema, source/admission partitions, complete coverage
  and delta closure, counterexample/failure/frontier views and recovery tests.
- **P3-D:** eligibility, consent, auditable random pairing, domain choice,
  bounded debate runtime, syntheses and quiet attention integration.
- **Later:** candidate-lesson review/promotion and calibrated complexity only
  after measured learning usefulness without work, privacy, cost or attention
  harm.

No current implementation timer, pairing, source-read expansion, new actor or
scheduled-review resumption follows from the design.
