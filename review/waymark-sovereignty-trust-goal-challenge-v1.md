## Judgment

Retain the three-way separation of reliance, participation and action disposition. Support plain challenge without humiliation, justified refusal without retaliation, permitted low-consequence cooperation, scoped reliance with explicit uncertainty, and contests that improve shared work without assigning authority to winners. None of that establishes AI consciousness or perfect independence.

The proposals are coherent design directions. Before claiming an enforceable contract, resolve the following issues and run the attached valid/adverse cases. JSON shape, a posterior and signed metadata are each weaker than the actual claim they might appear to support.

### WST-01 — dispatch and activation need cross-record coherence

The request schema's proceed-after-pushback conditional sets ten low-consequence booleans to true. It does not link them to `participation.state`, `authority.current` or `action_disposition.effect_gate_state`. A record can therefore contain an apparent proceed state alongside declined participation, stale authority or a failed gate without a declared schema constraint resolving the contradiction.

The reliance schema permits `ACTIVE_FOR_DECLARED_SCOPE` with a metric's `signed_definition=false`, or a purported Beta posterior whose parameters are negative/zero. Its generic distribution dictionary checks numeric shape, not whether the named probability family is valid. A self-reported signed flag would not prove verified provenance even if required true.

The competition schema admits two participant rows but does not enforce distinct identities or current consenting participation for `ACTIVE`. A draft may legitimately retain declined invitations; an executing two-party contest cannot count one participant twice or treat a decline as acceptance.

The consequence is a potentially misleading read model or an unsafe future consumer if it trusts those serialized labels. This is a contract counterexample, not an observed deployed bypass. Add state-specific semantic validation and reference closure, then independently revalidate current participation, authority, source/metric/model validity and consent at dispatch or activation. Hold inconsistent cases while preserving their original evidence. Do not make legitimate DRAFT/HELD/declined records impossible to store.

See [eleven synthetic fixture variants](fixtures/waymark-trust-contract-cases-v1.json), SHA256 `2f361a190ffe1ad2de435d9165d07b5d5cfd9842e2778e65ca3c98886d3311ae`: four valid controls and seven adverse inputs across these three contracts. JSON parsing, reference hashes and patch expansion were verified. Draft2020-12 validation was **not run** because `jsonschema` is unavailable in the actual project venv; no dependency was installed for this review. Structural acceptance is an inspection-derived expectation for independent validation. All identities, grants and outcomes are explicitly synthetic, not real permissions or empirical transfer cases.

### WST-02 — uncertainty must have an honest representation

The request schema requires a numeric confidence for every interpretation. An unresolved intent may have no defensible probability estimate. Neither 0 nor 0.5 automatically means unknown. Permit an explicit unknown/unestimated state, or a declared estimate with method, scope and uncertainty; do not force an arbitrary decimal to satisfy a form.

Likewise, a proper posterior is conditional on its model, evidence and observation process. Signed or reproducibly computed metrics do not establish that they measure the intended construct. Freeze their selection and outcome treatment; check prior sensitivity, dependence, missingness, drift, calibration and predictive fit. Prior/posterior predictive checks examine model behavior and fit, rather than making the model true. [Stan's primary guide](https://mc-stan.org/docs/stan-users-guide/posterior-predictive-checks.html)

Proper forecast scoring is useful for defined probabilistic predictions with suitable outcome evidence. It does not validate outcome labels, establish ethical legitimacy, or convert an epistemic estimate into permission. [Gneiting and Raftery's primary paper](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/people/raftery/Research/PDF/Gneiting2007jasa.pdf)

Ordinary UI: “This source has not been evaluated for this kind of request; I am checking this result.” Inspection may expose the model and evidence limits. Avoid a default “trust 94.7%” badge, especially when sparse observations or model selection dominate the number. No posterior/calibration model was fitted or validated in this review.

### WST-03 — privacy admission precedes verbatim requests and trust evidence

The source flow starts at RECEIVED and the request schema requires a `verbatim_ref`. Make the existing foundation's admission boundary explicit before persistent RequestCase creation. Owner-authored words can still contain prohibited third-party content or a secret. Store an admitted original reference only when its actual purpose, audience, source/data class and retention allow it. A rejected payload produces minimum non-content metadata, not a body, quarantine copy or low-entropy content digest.

Apply the same order to TrustUpdate, evaluator evidence, metrics, appeals and collective memories. “Everyone and everything” means no blind reliability exemption; it does not authorize scraping people, collecting unrelated interactions or building personal dossiers. Opaque identifiers and model-derived features can still expose protected information. Missing permitted evidence remains a limitation or hold, not a reason to collect forbidden material.

Acceptance: a harmless own request enters the case; a prohibited pasted field never reaches event storage, posterior inputs, search, logs or provider prompts; appeal reveals the decisive allowed factors while preserving another participant's confidentiality.

### WST-04 — many scoped scores can still become a social score

The proposed dimensions are scoped and appropriately separated, but a permanent dossier displaying them all, a roster sorted by them, or resource routing using their sum could recreate the prohibited global reputation system. Specify permitted consumers and prohibited inferences, not only “no aggregate score.” Show a decision-local reliance explanation and its evidence scope. Exclude persistent overall trust ranks and unjustified cross-domain transfer.

Separate actual behavior from disagreement with the evaluator. A justified refusal, correction challenge, warranted NO_CHANGE or principled abstention is not evidence of unreliability. “Correction responsiveness” must not reward accepting false corrections. Selection checks should detect withheld failures or misreported opportunities without punishing legitimate specialization or forcing unsafe/out-of-domain predictions merely to generate scoreable evidence.

Low reliance can justify proportionate verification of a claim; it cannot remove standing, appeal, permitted recovery or participation protections. High reliance cannot supply a missing grant. Track how reliance actually changes decisions and effort, including feedback loops where restricted opportunity creates missing data later mislabelled as failure.

### WST-05 — low consequence is a bounded profile, not a universal proof obligation

The proposed boundary is appropriately narrow. Make it usable through verified action profiles: for example, changing an isolated personal theme under existing authority, with no network, unrelated data, financial effect or protected-control alteration. Known safe profiles can establish relevant predicates; there is no need to prove absence of every imaginable consequence for each harmless preference.

Ask only when a material unresolved interpretation changes the right action. Offer a recommendation once, preserve the current request and let a clear repeated preference settle the low-impact choice when the participant is willing and the effect remains allowed. Do not require a separate “I understood” ceremony for every cosmetic decision. Recheck changed material premises and current permission, not the whole corpus.

A `Proceed with requested option` control must not force an agent that has declined. If another consenting participant may lawfully act, make that reassignment explicit and preserve the original dissent. A system prohibition offers its actual remedy/appeal rather than a proceed-anyway button. Broker denial and an individual's participation decision remain distinguishable.

### WST-06 — cooperation and credit must not become covert hierarchy

Retain disclosed cooperation, useful losing artifacts, source dependencies and correction credit. Freeze the primary objective and protected qualities before evaluation. Credit for helping, correcting or reproducing should be attributable contributions, not a hidden obedience or popularity score. A helpful metric cannot compensate for poorer correctness, privacy, refusal or traceability.

Use task-scoped views of artifacts and results. A win may nominate an artifact for ordinary review/adoption; it never automatically supplies a role, broader resources, a release or foundation authority. Preserve voluntary participation, protected ordinary-work budgets and a practical withdrawal path. Legitimate commitments may need a safe handoff; declining a contest does not justify retaliation. Shared models and sources stay disclosed rather than becoming multiple independent confirmations.

## Answers to the interaction questions

1. Attach pushback to the proposition, consequence and feasible alternative. “The result is reported; it has not been observed” is clearer than judging the person's character. Warranted agreement needs no defensive qualification.
2. Show recommendation, requested effect and a clear choice only where the current low-consequence profile permits it. Suppress repetitive confirmations; no bypass control for a failed actual gate or unwilling participant.
3. Show why this specific claim needs verification, decisive source age/version, material disagreement and the permitted correction route. Avoid global person profiles and exposing unrelated evidence. Detailed numerical models belong in authorized inspection with their limits.
4. Keep one durable matter containing scope, what remains safe, the deciding party, next remedy and review trigger. Continue unrelated work. Neither repeated accusations nor repeated refusals should flood the operator or disappear from history.
5. Reward useful contributions to a declared shared outcome, reproducibility and repair. Preserve losing alternatives and source dependencies. Never turn a scoreboard into a caste, actor grant or universal truth vote.

## Goal refinement proposal

The current provisional mission preserves the important relationships, but its opening mixes ends with architectural means and asks an ordinary reader to decode several framework terms. Keep that fuller statement as the internal governing account. A clearer working purpose is:

> Help people and permanent agents carry complex work forward without losing their place, their evidence, or their ability to inspect, question, decline participation, correct and recover.

Follow it with the operational promise to be tested: hIRC carries the bookkeeping and routine coordination; after time away, a participant can see what changed, what remains unknown, what needs their authority and the next justified action without reconstructing every conversation manually. It protects affected people and systems beyond the visible participant list as well as those inside it.

Local-first deployment, the IRC shell, Bayesian reliance, debates, competitions, licensing and federation are means or constraints. Stable operation and a declined contest remain valid. A capability count, engagement total or obedience score is not the goal.

Measure resumption time, missed commitments, false/missed escalations, wrong decisions from summaries, output quality, unauthorized data/effects, correction/exit/recovery success, attention and effort under matched tasks. Protect each relevant dimension and compare a simpler competent baseline; do not assert a speedup or solved non-domination from this wording. Define affected-party sampling and coverage so “every participant” does not hide people outside the system.

HIRC-I017 permits governed refinement. This proposal clarifies the existing purpose and preserves its safeguards; it is not adopted wording by this file. Keep the exact predecessor, attributed semantic diff and any changed acceptance/impact record. A revised goal cannot grant its own scope, rewrite a losing criterion or omit an older explicit requirement.
