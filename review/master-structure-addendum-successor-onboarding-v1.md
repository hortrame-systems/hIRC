# Master Plan 1.1 structure addendum — whole onboarding and succession

**Artifact ID:** HIRC-MASTER-STRUCTURE-SUCCESSOR-ONBOARDING-001  
**Status:** frozen candidate for UI-A challenge  
**Applies to:** HIRC-MASTER-STRUCTURE-CANDIDATE-001

## Foundation and boot sections

Open the architecture with one source-bound foundation, not a separate
onboarding feature. Add:

- a minimal `GenesisRoot` and owner-authorized genesis manifest for first
  activation, with ordinary work unavailable until successful activation;
- current catalog-derived `OnboardingSourceSet` and unit-level dispositions;
- a loss-aware compiler for invariants, judgment, formation, explanations,
  tests, workflows, history, conflicts and holds;
- a separate same-actor `FormationRepairLane` for proposed own storage and
  already admitted education/repair readers while incomplete; and
- runtime `FormationCase` states through scoped admission, with byte identity,
  comprehension, native identity, qualification, authority and action kept
  distinct.

The foundation stays active through ordinary work, provider calls, UI, memory,
licensing, debates, Bridge, release, recovery and succession. Protected source
text remains protected. Ordinary UI expresses behavior in the user's domain.

## New continuity section

Place **Permanent identity succession, transfer and deep sleep** after identity,
formation, authority and journals and before large-scale scheduling. It owns:

- exact authorized transfer trigger and idempotent case creation;
- predecessor smallest-safe-step completion and atomic generation/epoch freeze;
- minimized human/machine package plus signed manifest;
- fresh successor session, personal current formation and evidence-linked
  acceptance questions;
- acceptance before exact predecessor archive;
- separately observed archive, deep-sleep intent/confirmation, wake and tracker
  transitions;
- serial fleet order, shared-role dependencies and leader-last rule;
- red-watchdog scoped recovery with exact decision/verifier and negative
  dependency closure; and
- append-only correction, crash recovery and operator attention for ambiguous
  external effects.

No session, key, permission, attestation, memory/comprehension claim or completed
status transfers. No temporary actor path exists.

## Trusted interaction section

Consequential previews are built by the deterministic broker and displayed on a
trusted native/privileged surface outside the ordinary renderer. Approval and
dispatch bind the same current target/revision/recipient/data/cost/warning/
authority digest. The trusted surface remains accessible and preserves safe
inspection/recovery when the renderer is unavailable or suspected compromised.

## Memory and migration section

Keep the complete authorized migration manifest/coverage jobs separate from the
minimum active context sent to a runtime. Each partition has distinct rights and
processors. Provider-native opaque content, prohibited partitions, missing
coverage and late deltas remain explicit. A summary is not complete review and a
late delta cannot create two active effectful generations.

## Cross-domain rules

- Transfer/formation/sleep states are never idle-debate eligible.
- Continuity packages remain local unless a separately admitted Bridge contract
  permits exact fields and recipient; no authority crosses with them.
- Entitlement never blocks permitted first-party continuity evidence, safe
  read/export or contest/recovery, and entitlement keys are separate.
- Provider desired/sent/accepted/observed states remain separate for archive,
  sleep, wake and every other external effect.
- Foundation and continuity changes use predecessor-controlled release and
  recovery authority.

## Delivery sequence

- **P0:** GenesisRoot, exact source graph, dispositions, compiler reproducibility,
  boot/recovery and whole-source coverage tests.
- **P0-F:** same-actor formation repair, personal formation states, evidence,
  voice/admission and current-source transition tests.
- **P1:** generation/epoch fencing, trusted broker preview, atomic audit/outbox,
  key separation and late-writer denial.
- **P2-C:** transfer package, acceptance, archive/sleep/wake/tracker, serial fleet
  and scoped-red-recovery state machine with every-state crash fixtures.
- **P2-M:** complete migration manifest/jobs plus bounded runtime context and
  provider delta/opaque-state handling.
- **Later:** measured usability and recovery exercises before any broad
  succession use; Bridge transfer remains independently gated.
