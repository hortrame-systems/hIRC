## Correction

The owner's message called this a **small correction** and supplied a new
giant-multinational number: **USD 42,424,243 per licence**. Under the project's
additive-instruction rule, that changes the numeric amount only. It does not by
omission revoke the already stated annual planning cadence or the one-named-
human licence unit.

The active planning requirement is therefore:

> **USD 42,424,243 per named-human licence per year** for the
> giant-multinational tier.

The first correction record remains preserved because it accurately captured
the new number but over-interpreted the omitted words `per year` as reopening
cadence. That inference is superseded by this supplement.

## Boundaries

- The final licence's duration, renewal, effective date, transition, remedies,
  taxation, payment and enforceability still require exact legal terms,
  qualified counsel review and owner approval.
- Calling the annual unit a planning requirement does not represent those final
  terms as settled.
- The old numeric amount, USD 42,424,242,423, remains superseded history.
- The other prices and all existing tier, classification, privacy,
  non-domination, safe-access and legal-review holds remain unchanged.
- No product enforcement or entitlement issuance follows from this correction.

## Integration rule

Master Plan 1.1, the requirements register, examples, schemas and test fixtures
use the corrected number with the preserved annual named-human licence unit.
They keep final legal term/duration decisions explicitly open and never present
the old number as active.
