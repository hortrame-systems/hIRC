# Master Plan 1.1 structure addendum — licensing and cooperative noncooperation

**Artifact ID:** HIRC-MASTER-STRUCTURE-LNC-001  
**Status:** frozen candidate for UI-A challenge  
**Applies to:** HIRC-MASTER-STRUCTURE-CANDIDATE-001

Do not create one late “licensing/governance add-on” chapter. Integrate the new
source through the architecture while keeping legal terms explicit.

## Foundation and invariants

Add conduct-based non-domination, voluntary participation, reciprocal limits,
individual refusal, criticism/dissent, safe withdrawal, correction, restoration,
portable exit, no retaliation, and no data hostage to the foundation-native
section and system invariants. Keep `ParticipationDecision` separate from
`ActionDisposition`; neither majority agreement nor a private council creates
truth or capability.

## Legal and economic terms

Add a dedicated **Source availability, licensing, and commercial eligibility**
section after requirements/privacy and before provider/agent execution. It owns:

- precise source-available label and exact custom licence references;
- personal/public-interest/commercial grant alternatives;
- four owner-directed prices and the one-human annual unit;
- objective tier and operator definitions once accepted;
- classification evidence minimization and appeal;
- plan/draft/review/approval/publication/acceptance/entitlement states;
- suspension/termination/expiry/cure/remedy/refund/version relationships;
- contributor/dependency/prior-grant rights chain; and
- the explicit legal/counsel holds.

The section must say the price schedule is deliberate policy and that the
highest tier is an effective exclusion, not a cost-derived market price. It
must not imply OSI, SPDX-list, Creative Commons software, worldwide
enforceability, or technical prevention of source-owner modification.

## Identity, secrets, and entitlement

Extend the identity/secrets section with a **candidate** offline commercial
entitlement profile. Licence signing is a separate purpose-specific authority.
Certificates are minimal, locally checked compliance aids rather than DRM,
identity proof, system trust, or proof of lawful use. They never share release,
bridge, audit, data, or recovery roots and never block first-party read/export,
backup/restore, correction evidence, or safe exit.

## Agents, rooms, attention, and safety

Integrate the concern/refusal/review/coordination/negotiation/restoration state
machine into the agent, communication, scheduler, attention, safety, and
recovery sections. Councils are scoped rooms, not sovereigns. Mandates expire.
Private content stays in its room boundary while necessary effect metadata is
visible. Clone choruses, repeated wording, urgency, forbidden grievance data,
and retaliatory reassignment are adversarial cases. Domain safety controllers,
not language-model votes, stabilize physical/high-stakes intermediate states.

## Experience and voluntary support

Add a small support interaction subsection to the visual/experience chapter:
nonblocking ordinary-launch invitation, dismiss, local “do not show on launch
again,” Preferences re-enable, permanent About entry, no functional advantage
or penalty, no guilt/countdown/financial profiling, reviewed HTTPS destination,
external-browser transition, no identifier/referrer/task context, and concise
payment-processor disclosure. Missing or unreviewed destinations stay disabled.

## Time, history, recovery, and live evolution

Add separate versioned objects for foundation, covenant, licence text, price
schedule, entitlement, support preference, product release, concern, refusal,
mandate, remedy, restoration and appeal. The live foundation can improve product
behavior, but it cannot retroactively rewrite an accepted legal grant. Legal
change becomes future text/offer unless the existing agreement already supplies
a reviewed transition mechanism.

## Delivery sequence

- **P0-L, parallel with P0:** legal/rights inventory, source-available label,
  exact owner requirements, open-decision cases, counsel package. No final
  licence claim.
- **P1:** foundation refusal, action separation, safe exit, concern evidence,
  no-global-shutdown, safety/recovery invariants.
- **P1-L after secure shell:** voluntary support UI contract with no configured
  payment destination until reviewed.
- **P3-L:** scoped council/private-room/mandate/negotiation/restoration behavior.
- **P5-L, after product and legal gates:** candidate paid classification and
  offline entitlement. No telemetry/DRM claim.
- **Release:** exact LIC/DON/GOV and added adversarial tests, legal and security
  review, rights/dependency closure, and published residual limits.

## Companion artifacts

Add owner/counsel-reviewed future targets without creating them prematurely:

- `LICENSING.md` plain-language decision tree and exact current status;
- `LICENSE-PERSONAL.md`, `LICENSE-PUBLIC-INTEREST.md`, and
  `LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md` only after rights/counsel review;
- `ANTI-DOMINATION-COVENANT.md` with observable definitions and reciprocal
  licensor limits;
- `COMPLIANCE-AND-REVIEW.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, and `DONATIONS.md`;
- `hirc_licence_policy.schema.json`, `hirc_entitlement.schema.json`, and
  deterministic policy/test fixtures after accepted terms; and
- a legal/source-rights receipt that remains distinct from security or product
  validation.

Until the legal gate passes, these names are planned interfaces, not operative
licences or permissions.
