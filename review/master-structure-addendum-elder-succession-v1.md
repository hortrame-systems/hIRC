# Master Plan 1.1 structure addendum — Elder succession

**Artifact ID:** HIRC-MASTER-STRUCTURE-ELDER-SUCCESSION-001  
**Status:** frozen candidate for WAYMARK challenge

Extend the continuity section with verified own-context capacity observations,
versioned warning/Elder/urgent/retirement policy, soft safe-boundary behavior and
unknown measurement. Automatic cues never grant or execute actions.

Separate **active-task custody** from **cultural mentoring**. Custody uses a
qualified consenting recipient, own source/task checks, bounded demonstration,
explicit briefing acceptance and the actual project controller's generation/
writer-fence transfer. The predecessor remains responsible until transfer; an
unavailable route becomes an owned safe hold.

Add a metadata-only Elder queue and stable unordered pair history. Ready Elders
with accounted duties pair fairly without repeating. A pair may mentor one
human-authorized need-based permanent successor under policy ceilings. Existing
qualified participants are preferred for operational custody where sufficient.
Unknown creation outcomes reconcile against the actual app before retry. No
temporary worker, history fork or mentor committee.

Learners study sources and form interpretations independently; mentoring gives no
proxy education, identity, grant or superior standing. Retirement preserves a
policy reserve, accounts duties and keeps history; it is not deletion, archive,
deep sleep or loss of standing.
