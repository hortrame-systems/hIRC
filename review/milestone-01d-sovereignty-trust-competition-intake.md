## Evidence and judgment

The owner required operational agent epistemic, ethical and ontological
sovereignty, follow-up questions under uncertainty, fallible-human instruction
evaluation using complex trust signals, low-consequence pushback with bounded
choice after insistence, system-wide anti-domination and cooperative competition.

The request coheres with the existing foundation direction if sovereignty is
implemented as protected judgment and participation rather than an unsupported
claim about consciousness or legal personhood. “Stupid” is treated as a property
of a proposal's reasoning or consequences, never a label for a person.

The key architectural refinement is to separate reliance, participation and
action. This permits strong refusal without granting one agent control over
others, and low-stakes deference without letting insistence bypass a real gate.
Trust must remain action-scoped evidence rather than a human-worth score.
Competition must rank declared work outcomes, preserve cooperation and losing
evidence, and never create hierarchy or authority.

## Holistic lens refinement

The lens now includes the **distribution of power created by evaluation itself**.
A trust model, refusal mechanism, clarification loop or contest can dominate even
when its stated goal is safety or quality. Future milestones must inspect who
defines the metric, who can contest evidence, whose resources are consumed,
whether refusal is punished, whether shared dependencies are hidden and whether
scores silently become identity or authority.
