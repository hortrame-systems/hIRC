## Integrated assessment

The prior foundation candidate correctly made onboarding a runtime property, but
two missing layers became concrete:

1. a minimized predecessor-controlled genesis root is needed to establish the
   first valid foundation without circular self-authorization; and
2. an already authorized permanent participant needs a much narrower same-actor
   formation-repair lane to create its own proposed storage and use admitted
   education readers before it can complete formation.

Combining those routes would be unsafe. Genesis activates the first foundation.
Formation repair records honest incompleteness under that foundation and grants
no ordinary work. This distinction refines the bootstrap model.

Succession also cannot be a summarized memory copy. The durable unit is an
append-only transfer case binding exact generations, source/foundation versions,
minimized evidence, targeted acceptance and observed archive/sleep states.
Fresh personal formation, authority and execution epoch remain necessary. The
architecture therefore adds generation/epoch fencing and rejects stale
predecessor writes, concurrent active writers and package-to-readiness promotion.

The trusted-preview threat is now explicit. A canonical preview inside the
ordinary renderer is insufficient if that renderer is compromised. The broker
must create the exact intent and an actually independent trusted surface must
display it; approval and dispatch bind the same current digest.

Whole authorized migration study and active runtime context are separate
objects. Bounding runtime context must not silently narrow the owner's requested
whole in-scope reconciliation. Rights, processors, gaps and late deltas are
tracked independently.

The licensing correction is an exact source delta. The prior transfer value is
preserved as superseded history; the active value is USD 42,424,243 per licence.
Because the correction does not state a billing period, the architecture does
not silently retain the previous annual cadence. Commercial activation remains
held on the existing legal and definition questions.

## Holistic lens application

- **Evidence:** source selection, byte identity, reading, personal
  understanding, admission, authority, action and observed effect remain
  separate. Unknown package telemetry stays unknown rather than zero.
- **Reciprocal autonomy:** a transfer cannot impose identity, memory, ontology,
  permission or work on a successor. Decline, clarification, correction and
  recovery remain meaningful.
- **Purpose and consequence:** continuity preserves accountable work without
  hiding uncertainty or using speed to weaken privacy and acceptance.
- **Power and non-domination:** owner or supervisor trigger authority is scoped
  to continuity; no actor, majority, package or candidate compiler can promote
  itself or erase another generation's evidence.
- **Dependency and recovery:** boot root, live foundation, formation repair,
  continuity, release, Bridge and recovery keys/authorities are separated; safe
  inspection and export survive held states.

## Lens refinement

The previous lens treated formation, work and self-improvement as the main
foundation lifecycle. It now explicitly includes **genesis, incomplete formation
storage, succession, rest and intentional wake** as distinct authority-bearing
states. It also treats a correction to an economic directive as a typed source
delta with explicit supersession and unresolved semantics, rather than editing
history or inheriting nearby wording.
