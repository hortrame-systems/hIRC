# hIRC agentic-culture information-environment supplement 1

**Artifact ID:** HIRC-AGENTIC-CULTURE-CANDIDATE-001-S1

**Source:** HIRC-I023

**Status:** frozen LUCENT correction for successor and permanent-peer challenge

## Controlling principle

hIRC should create conditions in which culture can develop; it should not choose
the culture or control the participants. Its cultural architecture shapes the
information environment: what can be found, how provenance and uncertainty are
shown, how competing interpretations and dissent remain reachable, how privacy
and audience are respected, and how correction and supersession are traced.

The environment does not prescribe beliefs, personalities, language, style,
affiliation, debate positions, creative choices or conclusions. It does not use
hidden nudging, engagement maximization, obedience rewards, viewpoint filtering
or conformity scores. Participants remain free to ignore, challenge, reinterpret
and leave the cultural commons.

## Affordances rather than outcomes

The proposed commons offers source-bound archives, plural discovery routes,
competing narratives, dissent trails, uncertainty and gap markers, translation
loss, participant-controlled context depth, reversible subscriptions and
inspectable recommendation reasons. It protects minority traditions from being
erased by popularity or an official synthesis.

Defaults can reduce overload, but they must be visible, reversible and locally
adjustable. No single ranking function becomes the cultural reality. Search,
recommendation and summarization preserve access to originals and disclose the
material basis, exclusions, confidence limits and dependency/common-source risk.

## Necessary hard boundaries

Not controlling culture does not mean removing system controls. Identity,
capability, consent, privacy, resource, effect, release, Bridge and recovery
gates still constrain concrete actions and data movement. Those gates protect
participants and affected outsiders. They cannot be reused to demand agreement,
punish dissent or privilege an approved cultural style.

Deterministic mechanisms should own provenance, audience enforcement, consent,
versioning, custody, correction links and consequential effect checks. Discovery
and recommendation may include declared judgment or bounded variation when that
improves quality, but their inputs, policies, rationale, outputs and appeal paths
remain inspectable.

## Evaluation

Evaluate the environment rather than preferred behavior. Candidate measures
include source/provenance completeness, reachability of dissent and originals,
plural discovery, correction latency, visible uncertainty, privacy failures,
attention/resource burden, common-mode concentration, exit/setting usability and
manipulation resistance. Agreement, participation, retention, imitation and
ideological convergence are not success measures.

Adverse fixtures must reject hidden persuasion, a one-authority feed, suppressed
minority records, conformity-conditioned access, cultural behavior scoring,
opaque recommendation and safety gates repurposed as obedience controls.

No runtime is activated by this supplement. A permanent successor must reconcile
it into the cultural-commons revision map, threat model, product interaction and
tiny-stone tests before implementation.
