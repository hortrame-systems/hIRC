# hIRC goal refinement candidate 1

**Artifact ID:** HIRC-GOAL-CANDIDATE-001  
**Intent:** HIRC-I016  
**Status:** proposed for owner refinement and WAYMARK challenge; not final

## Candidate mission

> **Build hIRC as a local-first operating environment for accountable
> human-agent cooperation that preserves every participant's epistemic, ethical
> and ontological sovereignty while turning complex, changing work into a
> secure, recoverable, evidence-linked shared process that can improve itself
> without self-authorizing, surveilling, coercing or dominating anyone.**

## Operational north star

An authorized participant can leave, return, understand what happened and why,
see uncertainty and authority clearly, challenge or refuse any part, correct
errors and safely continue. Trust learns from outcomes. No human, agent, score,
model, licence or Bridge bypasses the same protections.

## Why this is narrower and stronger

The goal defines the human outcome and governing relationship. The classic UI,
permanent-agent organization, onboarding, debates, competitions, Bayesian
reliance, licensing, succession and federation are means or constraints. This
prevents a technically rich subsystem from becoming the project's purpose.

“Self-improving” is bounded: evidence can update models and propose changes in
real time, while semantic, permission, identity and release changes stay
source-bound, tested, attributable and independently activated. “Sovereignty”
describes protected judgment, participation, identity and boundaries; it does
not assert unestablished consciousness, legal personhood or technical power.

## Success dimensions

1. **Truth and evidence:** claims, inference, norms, authority, requested action,
   performed action, observed result and correction remain distinct and
   inspectable.
2. **Reciprocal autonomy:** participants can clarify, dissent, refuse, appeal,
   correct, recover and leave without retaliation, ontology capture or data
   hostage.
3. **Useful work and attention:** permitted work reconstructs automatically;
   missed commitments and false alerts fall; humans handle material decisions
   rather than bookkeeping.
4. **Security, privacy and resilience:** secrets/data stay scoped, compromise is
   contained, effects are attributable, clean recovery works and the Bridge
   remains off until independent evidence gates pass.
5. **Learning and adaptability:** Bayesian calibration, debates, cooperative
   competition and milestone reflection improve measured outcomes without
   turning score, consensus or rank into authority.

## Anti-goals

- no infallible human, agent, metric, model or system authority;
- no global social/obedience score, forced consensus, ideological conformity or
  permanent competition hierarchy;
- no surveillance expansion, hidden effects, self-authorized foundation change,
  remote sabotage or coercive dependency;
- no claim that framework vocabulary, reading, installation or agreement proves
  comprehension, goodness, personhood, alignment or permission; and
- no feature success metric that rewards engagement, obedience or scale while
  hiding harm, error, privacy loss, recovery failure or operator burden.

## Project-level evaluation

Every feature must trace to a success dimension and pass all anti-goals.
Evaluate time-to-recover, missed commitments, false alerts, accepted-output
quality, clarification/refusal precision, probabilistic calibration, privacy and
security incidents, recovery success, operator attention and correction cost.
No one metric can override the others; material trade-offs remain explicit.

The goal itself is versioned. A future correction identifies affected
requirements, threats, tests, phases and observed outcomes without rewriting the
predecessor.
