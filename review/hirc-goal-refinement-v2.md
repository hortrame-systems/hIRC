## Plain purpose

> **Help people and permanent agents carry complex work forward without losing
> their place, their evidence, or their ability to inspect, question, decline
> participation, correct and recover.**

## Operational promise

hIRC carries bookkeeping and routine coordination so that, after time away, a
participant can see what changed, what remains unknown, what needs their
authority and the next justified action without reconstructing every
conversation manually.

## Full governing account

hIRC is a local-first, secure, accountable and evidence-linked environment for
human-agent cooperation. It protects reciprocal epistemic, ethical and
ontological sovereignty; affected people and systems beyond the visible
participant list; finite attention; privacy; refusal; correction; recovery; and
governed improvement without self-authorization, surveillance, coercion or
domination.

## Semantic diff

Retained:

- all five prior success dimensions and anti-goals;
- evidence/authority/action separation and reciprocal autonomy;
- local-first security, privacy, resilience and governed learning;
- operational sovereignty without unsupported consciousness/personhood claims;
- interface, Bayesian reliance, debate, competition, licensing, succession and
  Bridge as means or constraints; and
- versioned future refinement through GoalChange.

Clarified:

- the participant outcome now precedes architectural language;
- affected outsiders and finite attention are explicit;
- stable operation and declined participation are valid;
- a simpler competent baseline is part of evaluation; and
- engagement, scale, obedience, feature/capability count and scores cannot stand
  in for the goal.

No participant, scope, data, feature, phase or action authority is added. No
prior owner requirement is cancelled.

## Evaluation

Use matched tasks and affected-party coverage to measure resumption time, missed
commitments, false/missed escalation, wrong decisions from summaries, accepted
output quality, unauthorized data/effects, correction/exit/recovery success and
attention/effort. Preserve protected dimensions separately; one metric cannot
offset a privacy, security, non-domination or recovery failure.
