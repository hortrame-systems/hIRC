- The system has one causal foundation rather than philosophical, onboarding,
  security and continuity add-ons.
- Human requests, factual reliance, agent participation, authority and system
  action are structurally separated.
- Trust and competition can learn from outcomes without directly minting
  permission, rank or truth.

## Lens refinement

The previous lens emphasized how every subsystem inherits the foundation. The
integrated draft reveals a complementary requirement: **governance depth cannot
erase product specificity**. A secure, non-dominating system still fails if
ordinary navigation, communication, work recovery, accessibility, scheduling or
provider behavior becomes vague. Future review must test semantic preservation
from both directions: foundation-to-feature and predecessor-feature-to-current
contract.

Metrics are also power-bearing architecture. Priors, eligibility, evaluation,
ranking and attention rules can dominate while appearing neutral. Each must
expose its construct, evidence, uncertainty, affected parties, challenge route
and prohibited authority effects.
