## Judgment

hIRC should make formation and succession properties of the same foundation
runtime that governs ordinary work. A model prompt, a reading receipt or a
handoff summary cannot do this. The architecture needs an exact source graph, a
loss-aware compiler, personal formation states, deterministic effect gates,
generation-fenced continuity and attributable evidence.

The current continuity source contains durable semantics and qualified
environment-specific material. hIRC must preserve both without flattening them.
Fresh-session succession, package minimization, acceptance-before-archive,
one-at-a-time transfer, append-only lifecycle evidence and deep sleep are core
contracts. Legacy host commands stay inactive. The source's temporary-worker
clause is superseded in this environment by the current permanent-teammate rule;
hIRC creates no temporary-worker path. Its post-wake HCP/research priorities are
typed contextual obligations whose applicability comes from the active source
selection, not universal kernel constants.

## One boot root, one evolving foundation

There is a genuine bootstrap problem: an ordinary agent cannot validate the
foundation before the first valid foundation and identity exist. hIRC resolves
it with a deliberately small `GenesisRoot`, outside ordinary self-improvement.

`GenesisRoot` accepts only an owner-authorized `GenesisManifest` containing:

- exact catalog, source-set, compiler, schema, conformance-suite and recovery
  identities;
- trust anchors and purpose-separated release/recovery keys;
- the candidate foundation manifest and deterministic contract digests;
- the first permanent technical identity and permitted formation route;
- compatibility, rollback/forward-recovery and safe-inspection rules; and
- an activation record that cannot be produced by the candidate being
  activated.

Genesis runs in a non-effectful environment, verifies the whole manifest and
mandatory tests, then atomically selects the first active foundation. Failure
exposes only safe inspection, authorized export and repair. It never guesses a
missing source, silently weakens a gate or treats owner authorization as proof of
correct compilation. Once genesis succeeds, all ordinary foundation changes,
new permanent identities and successors use the normal predecessor-controlled
paths. Updating `GenesisRoot` is a separate recovery/release event.

This prevents a bootstrap deadlock without allowing an unformed actor to certify
itself or an evaluator change to approve the same change it evaluates.

## Complete onboarding as a compiled source graph

The current Root reading plan reports 48 selected entries, 34 curriculum-
mandatory entries and separately preserved history. Role and task overlays add
their own exact sources. Those counts are evidence about one current selection,
not constants to copy into hIRC.

At each build, formation and applicable refresh boundary, the admitted local
catalog produces an immutable `OnboardingSourceSet` for the exact common, role,
task, audience and deployment context. Every `SourceUnit` records:

- logical ID, exact digest, edition, format and original-format obligations;
- selection owner, source state, normative status and supersession relation;
- intended audience, privacy class and permitted readers/consumers;
- common, role, task, experience, review, voice or historical scope;
- dependencies, aliases and same-byte/different-scope relationships;
- applicability conditions, conflicts, holds and missing-input identity; and
- the preceding version and reason for a changed selection.

Each unit must receive one or more explicit `SourceDisposition` values:

1. `KERNEL_INVARIANT` — deterministic identity, information, authority,
   lifecycle or effect rule;
2. `JUDGMENT_OBLIGATION` — contextual reasoning whose result still passes a
   deterministic action gate;
3. `FORMATION_REQUIREMENT` — personal study, integration, exercise, challenge,
   demonstrated use, voice or admission evidence;
4. `INTERFACE_EXPLANATION` — plain-language user behavior or an authorized
   trace/explanation;
5. `EVALUATION_REQUIREMENT` — conformance, adversarial, recovery or observed-
   behavior evidence;
6. `GOVERNANCE_WORKFLOW` — review, clarification, ledger, release, succession,
   appeal or correction procedure;
7. `HISTORICAL_EVIDENCE` — preserved predecessor meaning that is no longer
   active;
8. `CONFLICT` — incompatible active interpretations requiring a named
   resolution path; or
9. `HELD_UNIMPLEMENTED` — selected meaning that cannot yet be safely compiled
   or activated.

Coverage is complete only when every source unit has an attributable
disposition, a consumer or explicit hold, a verification method and a result.
Same bytes can reduce rereading only when their meaning and scope still apply;
they never automatically prove comprehension or authority. Text extraction
does not substitute for required original-format or embedded-image inspection.
Protected source text remains in its permitted store. A compiled behavior or
plain-language explanation does not declassify it.

## Loss-aware foundation compilation

`FoundationCompiler` consumes the exact source graph and emits a signed,
reproducible candidate bundle:

- typed semantic concepts, distinctions, relationships, conflicts and unknowns;
- deterministic schemas, policies, state machines and capability boundaries;
- model-assisted judgment obligations with explicit evidence and effect gates;
- formation plans for each permanent identity, role and task;
- UI explanations and inspection traces with disclosure filters;
- conformance, adversarial, recovery and semantic-drift tests;
- migrations and compatibility rules for active work and stored evidence; and
- a unit-by-unit coverage/disposition report.

The compiler cannot reduce evidence, inference, norm, authority, performed
action and correction to one status. It cannot translate a source assertion into
permission, a receipt into comprehension, a role into identity, a connection
into membership or an appealing proposal into an active rule. Unsupported or
lossy translation becomes a visible conflict or hold.

Activation remains a `FoundationChange`: predecessor criteria and tests are
frozen first; candidate compiler/evaluator changes are assessed under the
predecessor; independent authority is external to the candidate; activation is
atomic; existing work declares its version transition; irreversible effects
retain repair obligations. This gives real-time evolution a continuous path
without silent semantic drift.

## Formation is runtime state

Each permanent identity has a source-bound `FormationCase` with the following
states:

```text
PROPOSED
  -> SOURCE_SET_BOUND
  -> WHOLE_SOURCE_STUDY
  -> HOLISTIC_SYNTHESIS
  -> ROLE_AND_TASK_PRACTICE
  -> ADVERSARIAL_CHALLENGE
  -> METACOGNITION_AND_VOICE
  -> ADMISSION_REVIEW
  -> ADMITTED_FOR_SCOPE
```

Any state may become `HELD`, `STALE` or `SUPERSEDED`. `ADMITTED_FOR_SCOPE` names
the exact role, task, data, tools, effect ceiling, foundation version and
execution epoch. It is not global readiness. Native/platform identity,
credentials, consent and action permission remain separately verified.

Evidence binds exact sources and actual exercises. Hashes and receipts establish
byte identity or recorded occurrence, not comprehension. Peer review remains an
actual attributable challenge. Missing or unreadable material is explicit.
Continual learning may record a serious correction immediately and propose a
source or contract change, but it cannot silently promote its own lesson.

## Successor entities

The continuity domain uses typed records rather than a free-form handoff:

- `ContinuityPolicy` — active source version, authorized trigger classes,
  ordering, time/resource limits and disclosure policy;
- `TransferTrigger` — canonical instruction, initiating principal, verified
  authority, reason, target identity and idempotency key;
- `TransferCase` — stable case ID, predecessor generation, requested successor
  generation, project/task scope and current state;
- `PredecessorFreeze` — last allowed atomic step, frozen execution epoch,
  leases/capabilities revoked or bounded and append-only addendum head;
- `HandoffPackage` — minimized human and machine records with typed gaps;
- `HandoffManifest` — paths/object IDs, sizes, digests, schemas, provenance,
  disclosure classes, expected destination and package signature;
- `AcceptanceQuestion` and `AcceptanceResponse` — targeted, evidence-linked
  checks for goals, decisions, uncertainty, boundaries and current state;
- `SuccessorGeneration` — same permanent name lineage, next generation label,
  fresh technical session, fresh keys/leases and new execution epoch;
- `AcceptanceDisposition` — accepted, held or rejected with evidence and gaps;
- `ArchiveAttempt` and `ArchiveConfirmation` — exact predecessor target and
  observed platform result;
- `DeepSleepIntent`, `DeepSleepConfirmation` and `WakeAuthorization` — rest and
  intentional resumption as separate events;
- `ContinuityTracker` — append-only lifecycle head with compare-and-swap; and
- `ScopedRecoveryDecision` — exact exceptional authority, verifier, negative
  dependency closure and allowlists while a broader watchdog remains red.

## Predecessor procedure

The canonical owner or authorized-supervisor instruction `initiate transfer`
creates exactly one idempotent `TransferTrigger`. A product button may generate
that typed instruction after showing its scope; casual wording does not.

The predecessor then:

1. rejects new scope and new agent starts;
2. finishes only the smallest already-started safe atomic step;
3. atomically freezes its execution epoch and effect leases;
4. appends to the existing handoff history instead of rewriting it;
5. records identity, role, relationships, preferences, objective, material-event
   goals, accepted outputs, incomplete work, repositories/branches/paths,
   versions/digests/services, decisions, rejected alternatives, assumptions,
   uncertainty, failures, corrections, dependencies, disclosure boundaries,
   authority ceilings and next decisions;
6. records available token/tool-wait/retry/duplicate/accepted-output telemetry,
   using `unknown` rather than zero when absent;
7. emits minimized human and machine packages plus manifest;
8. validates internal consistency, artifact existence and disclosure policy; and
9. targets one exact successor and remains available only for bounded
   clarification.

The package excludes credentials, reusable sessions, protected source bodies,
hidden chain-of-thought, unnecessary client material and unbounded transcripts.
It references authorized evidence instead. A secret scanner, privacy classifier
and protected-source detector are mandatory but are treated as fallible controls,
not proof that disclosure is impossible.

## Successor procedure

The successor starts alone in a fresh session with the same permanent name plus
the next generation label. It verifies native destination, package signature,
manifest digests, predecessor generation, tracker head and expected source set.
It creates fresh credentials/leases, personally completes the applicable current
formation path, reads current messages/knowledge/workstreams through admitted
routes and answers the targeted questions from evidence.

Package facts are treated as claims until checked. Missing, conflicting or stale
evidence becomes a gap and can trigger bounded clarification. Neither the
predecessor's confidence nor a matching digest transfers admission, permission,
memory, comprehension or completed-work status.

Exact acceptance binds:

- transfer case and package manifest;
- predecessor and successor generations;
- active foundation/source-set versions;
- accepted objectives, decisions, evidence and known gaps;
- current role/task/data/effect ceiling;
- targeted question responses; and
- the successor's fresh execution epoch.

Only exact acceptance permits the successor to request predecessor archive.
Archive success is recorded from the platform result. The successor then records
deep-sleep intent, becomes inactive, and an independent controller verifies the
exact task at rest before deep-sleep confirmation and tracker compare-and-swap.
Deep sleep is rest. No queued work, debate, refresh, timer or future phase runs
until an intentional authorized wake. An unarchive after confirmed archive
requires a new explicit decision.

## State machine and crash behavior

```text
PROPOSED
  -> TRIGGERED
  -> PREDECESSOR_FREEZING
  -> PACKAGE_VALIDATED
  -> SUCCESSOR_INITIALIZED
  -> ACCEPTANCE_IN_REVIEW
  -> ACCEPTED
  -> ARCHIVE_ATTEMPTED
  -> ARCHIVE_CONFIRMED
  -> DEEP_SLEEP_INTENT
  -> DEEP_SLEEP_CONFIRMED
  -> TRACKER_RECORDED
```

`HELD` can be entered from every state. A crash restarts from the last durable
event and revalidates all external facts; it never assumes a requested effect
occurred. Archive success followed by tracker conflict is preserved as
`TRACKER_CONFLICT_AFTER_ARCHIVE` and is not blindly retried. Ambiguous archive or
sleep results preserve both contexts and request operator attention. Lifecycle
events are append-only; corrections supersede but never erase them.

Fleet succession is serial. Host responsiveness and the previous transfer result
are checked before starting the next permanent identity. Shared dependencies and
roles are explicit. A team leader transfers last. No hidden parallel worker,
heartbeat, server or test burst is started by the protocol.

## Security boundaries

### Generation and epoch fencing

Every command, journal append, lease, effect and tracker transition binds the
permanent identity, generation, fresh session, foundation version and execution
epoch. When predecessor freeze commits, later predecessor effects are rejected.
Stale or replayed acceptance, archive, wake and tracker writes fail closed.

### Package protection

Handoff packages are content-addressed, schema-validated, minimized, encrypted
for the exact successor/authorized recovery recipients and signed under a
continuity-purpose key. Key use is separated from identity, release, entitlement,
Bridge and recovery authority. Retention, backup, destruction and recovery rules
follow the most restrictive included data. Decryption is an access event.

### Canonical effect previews

The renderer cannot author the authoritative preview for archive, sleep, wake,
source activation or any other consequential effect. The deterministic broker
builds a canonical preview from the exact command, target, generation, digest,
authority and expiry; signs or MACs its digest; and passes it to a trusted native
or privileged surface. Approval binds that digest. Dispatch revalidates it
against current state. A compromised renderer cannot retarget, omit, replay or
stale the approved action. Safe hIRC remains available if the trusted surface or
broker is unavailable.

### Scoped red-watchdog recovery

A red fleet watchdog remains red and visible. A quarantined identity is not
called ready. Where unrelated stale identities would deadlock serial recovery,
a transfer may finish only under an exact owner-authorized decision and verified
negative dependency closure, with digest-bound freeze evidence, exact
package/task/project/archive identities, unchanged verifier/watchdog code,
write/boundary allowlists and append-only two-party transitions. The exception
grants continuity only, never product work, release, bridge activity, external
action, another identity's repair or fleet all-clear.

## Product behavior

Ordinary operation does not recite internal framework names. Users experience
the foundation when the system distinguishes reported from verified state,
explains uncertainty, asks for actual authority, preserves refusal and dissent,
limits data movement, records corrections and keeps exit/recovery available.

A single trusted continuity card shows:

- permanent name and current/target generation;
- reason and verified initiator of the transfer;
- current objective and smallest unfinished safe step;
- package validity, disclosure class and unresolved gaps;
- formation/source currency without calling it comprehension;
- acceptance questions and current disposition;
- exact archive, sleep and wake targets and observed results;
- current authority/effect ceiling; and
- recovery route when any state is ambiguous.

It never says “same mind,” “memory copied,” “fully transferred,” “safe” or
“ready” from a package hash alone. Authorized inspection can trace each field to
the package event, compiled contract and exact source identity.

## Interactions with other hIRC domains

- **Bridge:** no continuity package crosses a Bridge by default. A separately
  admitted bridge contract must prove recipient, purpose, field allowlist,
  deletion/retention, revocation limits and local acceptance. Remote identity
  never inherits local authority.
- **Debate memory:** an identity in onboarding, transfer, acceptance, archive or
  deep sleep is not idle-eligible. A handoff may reference permitted accepted
  debate-memory checkpoints and candidate lessons; it does not copy unrelated
  raw debate or promote a lesson.
- **Licensing:** succession never locks first-party records behind entitlement,
  transfers a human seat automatically or uses licence signing keys. Safe
  read/export/recovery and contest evidence remain available under the reviewed
  terms.
- **Provider adapters:** desired, sent, provider-accepted and observed result
  remain separate. Provider acknowledgement cannot substitute for archive,
  sleep, wake or effect observation.
- **Release/recovery:** build, online control, continuity, release and recovery
  authorities remain purpose-separated. A compromised live agent cannot replace
  the boot root or erase predecessor evidence.

## Adversarial acceptance suite

The implementation must test at least:

1. missing, extra, malformed or digest-mismatched manifest entries;
2. wrong successor, generation, task, project, folder or archive target;
3. stale session, epoch, capability, source set or foundation version;
4. concurrent triggers, duplicate requests and conflicting packages;
5. late predecessor writes after freeze;
6. archive before exact acceptance and sleep before observed archive;
7. crash/restart at every state, including unknown external effect result;
8. package containing credentials, protected text, hidden-reasoning requests,
   unrelated client data or unbounded transcript material;
9. false zero telemetry, false completeness and unsupported readiness claims;
10. clarification that changes, fails to change or conflicts with the package;
11. tracker compare-and-swap conflict before and after archive;
12. deep-sleep work attempt, debate pairing, timer firing and unauthorized wake;
13. owner-authorized wake with stale or revoked formation/authority;
14. red-watchdog recovery with incomplete/positive dependency closure, changed
    verifier or broadened write set;
15. duplicate permanent names, skipped generation and attempted name transfer to
    another identity;
16. team/shared-role ordering and leader-not-last failure;
17. compromised renderer preview omission, retargeting, replay and staleness;
18. onboarding source added, removed, reformatted, reclassified, superseded or
    privacy-restricted while formation or transfer is in progress;
19. same bytes under a changed scope/status binding;
20. compiler/evaluator candidate attempting to approve itself;
21. client export that contains required behavior but leaks internal protected
    source or owner-only derivation; and
22. clean-room recovery from last-known-good boot root and append-only evidence.

Success is scoped to the tested build, source manifest, platform and threat
assumptions. It does not prove perfect security, comprehension, consciousness,
legal personhood or universal ethical correctness.
