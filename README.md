# hIRC

hIRC helps people and permanent agents carry complex work forward without
losing their place, evidence, or ability to inspect, question, decline
participation, correct and recover.

hIRC is being reconciled from the owner-supplied design transfer into a
foundation-native, security-first, evidence-linked master plan.

- [Review evidence and ledger](review/README.md)
- [Master Plan 1.1 working drafts](drafts/README.md)
- [Milestone and Git checkpoints](milestones/README.md)
- [Executable v0.1 implementation](implementation/README.md)

The owner transfer archives are preserved as local inputs and deliberately
excluded from Git; their exact hashes and review boundaries are recorded in the
ledger. Current architecture and status must be read from the intent register,
review ledger, and latest accepted master-plan revision rather than inferred
from filenames.
