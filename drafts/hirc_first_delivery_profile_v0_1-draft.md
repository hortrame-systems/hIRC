# hIRC first delivery profile 0.1 — working draft

**Status:** candidate delivery boundary pending integrated Milestone 03 challenge
and final disposition

**Purpose:** prove useful local work recovery under the foundation/security core
without activating optional high-risk or unvalidated subsystems

## 1. Outcome

The first delivery is successful when one authorized local human can use one
secure hIRC installation to work with a small admitted set of permanent agent
identities, leave, return and recover:

- what changed;
- current commitments and dependencies;
- reported versus observed results;
- uncertainty and coverage gaps;
- the few decisions that genuinely need human authority;
- the next justified action and evidence; and
- a safe correction/recovery path.

It must reduce matched-task resume effort and missed commitments against a
simpler competent baseline without increasing unauthorized data/effects, false
confidence, alert burden or recovery failure.

## 2. What is baked in from the first schema

Even when a later feature is disabled, the first persistent and runtime schemas
already include:

- foundation, GoalVersion and source-set identities;
- identity, role, generation, fresh session and execution epoch;
- evidence/inference/norm/authority/action/correction separation;
- requested/sent/provider-accepted/observed effect states;
- privacy class, purpose, source, retention, egress and derived-data inheritance;
- RelianceDecision, ParticipationDecision and ActionDisposition separation;
- refusal, clarification, appeal and non-retaliation events;
- atomic revision/audit/outbox and idempotency;
- compatible succession/formation state references;
- cultural artifact, discovery, participation and environment-version identities
  with explicit disabled/default-off states and no authority effect;
- resource and operator-attention budgets; and
- append-only correction/supersession.

This avoids a later destructive migration from an unconstrained “ordinary app”
into the intended system. Disabled features have typed states, no dormant grants
or network listeners.

## 3. Enabled scope

### Foundation and formation

- verified GenesisRoot and one active foundation bundle;
- complete source-unit disposition at the declared first-delivery scope;
- same-actor FormationRepairLane;
- personal formation/admission for the permanent identities actually used;
- GoalVersion 2 and governed change records; and
- Safe hIRC inspection/export/repair.

### Local core

- one local human principal profile, pending owner decision to expand;
- durable permanent agent registry, scoped roles and no temporary-agent path;
- typed request/authority/participation/action pipeline;
- privacy admission before storage and egress;
- local source/event ingestion with explicit coverage;
- canonical work, commitments, dependencies and corrections;
- journal/outbox, audit anchors, execution epochs and recovery;
- secret broker and purpose-separated first-delivery keys; and
- trusted consequential preview surface outside the ordinary renderer.

### Useful work loop

- classic IRC-style rooms/direct chat and compact navigation;
- Back/Forward/Resume, drafts, scroll/task bookmark and one attention desk;
- one exact provider/model route after bounded capability/privacy probe;
- non-effectful analysis first, then a narrowly allowlisted local/synthetic
  effect class with idempotency and observed-result checks;
- provider outage/unknown-result recovery;
- automatic work reconstruction and evidence drilldown; and
- tested local archive/restore and clean-room recovery for this profile.

## 4. Disabled or held subsystems

### Bridge

No listener, public advertisement, peer enrollment, remote payload or Bridge
effect. The four-component interfaces and field classifications exist in design
and schemas only. Activation follows the seven independent gates.

### Debates and cooperative competitions

Policies, eligibility fields, memory/charter namespaces and no-authority
invariants may exist. No scheduler pairs agents, no debate/contest executes and
no background timer runs. These start only after ordinary work, privacy,
attention and resource floors have measured evidence.

Plural discovery, cultural subscriptions and environment measures also remain
disabled. Their schemas preserve provenance, dissent, direct-source escape,
default-off consent, safe exit, metric missingness and prohibited behavioral
proxies so later activation does not require an unconstrained migration. No
culture/person score or cultural telemetry is collected.

### Bayesian trust

Requests can record `UNESTIMATED` reliance or source-specific evidence. No
global score, roster sorting or production posterior changes access/resources.
Bayesian models activate by dimension only after metric/evaluator/privacy/model-
fit validation. Deterministic identity/authority/effect gates remain sufficient
for the first work loop.

### Commercial licensing and support payment

The product can display accurate planning/legal status and source-available
notices. No paid classification, entitlement certificate, payment destination,
enforcement, suspension or termination. A support prompt remains disabled until
its neutral UI and destination/privacy review pass.

### Extensions and arbitrary hScript

Only built-in signed functionality. Import/install UI may be absent or accept
packages into a disabled inspection state. No third-party extension execution or
agent UI modification.

### Live foundation self-improvement

Evidence, corrections and candidate Goal/FoundationChange records may be
created. No automatic material activation. The first release uses deliberate
predecessor-controlled review, tests and explicit activation.

### Fleet succession

All identities and work are generation-aware and transfer schemas/events exist.
The profile records source-qualified context-capacity observations and can keep a
compact continuity record without polling or treating a cue as authority.
An end-to-end archive/deep-sleep transfer is not a release blocker for the first
single-generation useful loop, but it is required before the profile is used for
context-replacement operations or a second production generation.

## 5. Minimum trusted UI

1. **Resume:** prior context, changed work, gaps, unknown outcomes and next
   justified action.
2. **Room/direct conversation:** exact route/participants/data scope and source/
   observation state.
3. **Work/evidence:** commitments, dependencies, acceptance, correction and
   source drilldown.
4. **Decision card:** proposition/action, evidence/uncertainty, affected parties,
   authority, agent participation, alternatives and exact consequence.
5. **Trusted preview:** target, recipient, data, cost/warning, authority, expiry
   and intent digest.
6. **Security/recovery:** independent unsummarized queue, source coverage,
   archive/restore health and Safe hIRC.

The UI uses plain purpose language. Internal source/contract terminology appears
only in authorized inspection.

## 6. Required first-delivery tests

### Work and evidence

- return-after-absence scenario reconstructs changes and next action;
- stale/missing source prevents all-clear;
- contradictory report and observed result remain separate;
- correction preserves predecessor and updates projections;
- duplicate source event is idempotent; and
- disabled cultural/discovery state cannot enroll, rank, suppress dissent, change
  standing/authority or remove direct-source access; and
- manual tracker upkeep is unnecessary for the tested workflow.

### Privacy and secrets

- prohibited pasted/client/third-party field never reaches case, log, index,
  provider or posterior evidence;
- derived summary retains source restriction;
- prompt/export/log/renderer scan finds no secret value; and
- egress preview matches actual payload/recipient/processor.

### Identity, authority and action

- spoofed, stale, expired and out-of-scope authority denied;
- authentic authority with false premise triggers correction, not truth;
- unwilling participant cannot dispatch;
- failed gate has no proceed-anyway route;
- eligible safe-profile preference avoids confirmation fatigue; and
- renderer retarget/replay/stale approval denied by broker/current-state check.

### Provider and recovery

- desired/sent/accepted/observed configuration/result remain distinct;
- timeout/unknown outcome reconciles before retry;
- unsupported setting does not disappear silently;
- provider outage preserves local work and suspended/unknown state;
- crash between journal/outbox/dispatch recovers without duplicate effect;
- stale execution epoch cannot commit; and
- archive/key/device/controller loss restores in an isolated clean environment.

### Foundation and supply chain

- missing/mismatched source/disposition/compiler/test/manifest blocks boot;
- candidate cannot approve its own evaluator/activation;
- protected source is absent from client/runtime deliverables;
- locked build/SBOM/provenance/artifact/update verification passes; and
- Safe hIRC starts with renderer/extensions/provider unavailable.

## 7. Go/no-go gate

Go requires:

- exact first-delivery source, build, configuration and threat/test trace;
- no unresolved high-severity path in the enabled scope;
- independent restoration from accepted backup/key custody;
- measured work-resume benefit without worse missed obligations or false alerts;
- usable clarification/refusal/trusted-preview flow;
- explicit residual risks and accountable owners; and
- rollback/forward-recovery and incident procedure.

No-go or hold applies if privacy admission, authority/effect enforcement,
journal/outbox atomicity, trusted preview, secret handling, recovery or honest
coverage/result states are missing. Disabled optional features do not block the
useful loop; accidentally active optional features do.
