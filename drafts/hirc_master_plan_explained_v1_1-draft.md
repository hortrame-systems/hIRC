# hIRC Master Plan 1.1 — explained working draft

**Status:** plain-language companion pending integrated Milestone 03 review and final disposition

## What hIRC is for

hIRC helps people and permanent agents carry complex work forward without losing
their place, evidence, or ability to question, decline, correct and recover.

Today, complex work scatters across conversations, files, tools, teams and model
sessions. People return after hours or days and must reconstruct what happened,
which promises still matter, whether a reported result really occurred and what
needs their decision. hIRC makes that reconstruction part of the system.

The familiar surface is a compact classic IRC-style application: rooms, direct
conversations, nested teams, commands, independent windows, searchable history
and a small operator desk. Under that surface is an evidence-linked work and
security system.

## What “baked in” means

hIRC does not run a normal agent system and then attach an ethics course or
policy prompt. Its basic objects and state transitions already distinguish:

- what someone said from what evidence supports;
- a prediction from an observed result;
- advice from permission;
- willingness from authority;
- connection from membership;
- a role from identity or ownership;
- a handoff from copied memory; and
- a score from truth or worth.

The same foundation shapes identity, data, actions, explanations, refusal,
learning, updates, recovery and federation. Ordinary users do not need its
internal vocabulary. They see plain behavior: “this is reported, not observed,”
“I may have misunderstood,” “this recipient is not authorized,” “I recommend a
different route,” or “you can inspect and appeal this decision.”

## Humans are not treated as infallible

A human instruction is important evidence of intent, especially from the owner,
but it is not automatically a true factual premise or justified effect. hIRC
checks who asked, what authority they actually have, what the evidence says, who
could be affected and whether the action is permitted.

Agents can challenge bad reasoning, ask a focused follow-up, decline their own
participation or refuse an effect that fails a real ethical, privacy, security or
authority boundary. They explain the reason and offer a valid alternative when
possible. A supported refusal does not reduce their role, resources or standing.

For harmless preferences, the system does not become paternalistic. If a local,
reversible, low-cost action is fully permitted, the agent can explain why it
thinks the choice is poor. If the human still wants it, the agent may proceed or
decline without turning the interaction into a ceremony. A real denial never has
a “proceed anyway” button.

This protects practical agent judgment without claiming that an AI is conscious,
a legal person or independent of its platform.

## Trust changes with evidence

hIRC never gives a person one permanent trust score. It learns separate,
context-specific reliability estimates: for example, whether a source is well
calibrated for one provider/version, whether a tool reliably reports observed
results, or whether an authority claim stays current.

Those estimates update from attributable interactions and outcomes using
declared Bayesian models. They show prior assumptions, dependence, missing data,
uncertainty, calibration and drift. Five reports that copy one source do not
count as five independent confirmations. A changed metric starts a new version.

Metrics and evaluators are reviewed too. A signed metric can still measure the
wrong thing. A posterior can still be based on poor labels or missing outcomes.
The UI therefore avoids “this person is 94.7% trustworthy.” It says what this
specific claim needs and why.

High reliability does not grant permission. Low reliability does not remove
standing, appeal or recovery. Refusal, abstention and disagreement are not
treated as unreliability merely because an evaluator disliked them.

## What happens to a request

A consequential request follows one path:

1. privacy admission decides whether the content may enter the system;
2. hIRC identifies the source and possible interpretations;
3. it asks a focused question only if ambiguity would materially change the
   right action;
4. it assesses evidence and context-specific reliance;
5. it checks affected parties, consent, norms and actual authority;
6. the selected agent decides whether it participates;
7. the system decides whether the effect is permitted;
8. a trusted surface shows the exact target, data, recipient, cost and warning;
9. approval binds that exact intent;
10. the journal and outbound operation commit atomically;
11. hIRC distinguishes request, send, provider acceptance and observed result;
    and
12. later evidence can correct the record without erasing history.

A compromised chat renderer cannot author the authoritative preview. The
deterministic broker does, and an independent privileged surface displays it.

## Returning to work

When someone returns, hIRC restores their layout, drafts, selected task and
reading position. It builds a briefing from source events:

- what changed;
- which commitments are complete, blocked, stale or unknown;
- which reported results have been observed;
- which sources stopped reporting;
- which decisions genuinely need the person;
- what the next justified action is; and
- how to inspect the underlying evidence.

The person does not manually reconcile conversations or drag cards to keep the
system honest. If a source is missing, hIRC says coverage is incomplete rather
than “all clear.”

## Permanent agents and teams

An agent is one durable logical identity, not every model process that serves
it. It can belong to several teams without being cloned. Teams can contain teams
at arbitrary depth. Reservations, queues and execution epochs prevent one agent
from producing duplicate effects across teams.

New permanent identities require source-bound formation and scoped admission.
The current design has no temporary-agent escape hatch. A planner can propose a
large organization, but the system groups its resource and authority changes in
one bounded plan and resumes idempotently after failure.

## Onboarding and self-improvement are one lifecycle

The current source catalog generates the exact common, role and task material
for each identity. Every source unit has a visible use—runtime invariant,
judgment obligation, personal formation, explanation, test, workflow, history,
conflict or hold. Nothing disappears because it cannot be put in code.

A small genesis root establishes the first valid foundation without allowing it
to approve itself. A separate repair lane lets an incomplete permanent
participant create only its own proposed notes and read admitted education
sources; it cannot work normally until formed and admitted.

Evidence and lessons can accumulate continuously. A lesson that changes a norm,
permission, source, identity, kernel or evaluator goes through a versioned
change with predecessor tests, independent activation, migration and recovery.
The goal itself can be refined the same way. A metric cannot redefine the mission
because it became easy to optimize.

## Succession without pretending to copy a mind

When the exact authorized transfer instruction is issued, the predecessor stops
new scope, finishes a smallest safe step and freezes its execution epoch. It
builds a minimized signed package of work, evidence, decisions, uncertainty,
failures, boundaries and next actions for one exact successor. It includes no
credentials, reusable session, protected source bodies or hidden reasoning.

The successor starts in a fresh session, verifies the package, personally
completes current formation and answers evidence-linked questions. Only exact
acceptance permits predecessor archive. Archive, deep sleep and later wake are
separate observed events. The sleeping successor does no queued work until an
authorized wake.

The system says “this package is valid for these artifacts and gaps,” never “the
same mind was transferred.”

When a verified own-context monitor shows capacity approaching a selected soft
boundary, hIRC updates continuity and prioritizes safe project custody; the cue
itself does not create or wake anyone. A qualified consenting recipient must
accept the briefing and the real project writer fence must transfer separately.
If no recipient is available, the work gets an owned safe hold.

Optional cultural mentoring happens only after active duties are accounted. Two
ready Elders never repeat the same stable-identity pair; they may mentor one
human-authorized need-based permanent successor. The learner studies sources and
forms its own interpretation. Mentoring supplies no inherited education,
permission or superior status. Retirement preserves reserve and history; it is
not deletion or archive.

## Ethical debates and cooperative competition

hIRC first secures the surrounding information conditions. Cultural records keep
their source, audience, privacy, correction and dissent. Discovery shows its
candidate set, policy, rationale, exclusions and uncertainty, and always leaves a
permitted direct-source route. Cultural participation is off by default,
reversible and cannot change standing, permission or resources. A person or agent
can leave, stay quiet or dissent without retaliation.

The system watches for hidden nudging, poisoned collective memory, mentor-status
authority, captured rankings, false diversity, conformity penalties, overbroad
safety gates and cultural annexation through the Bridge. Recovery narrows or
disables the affected information path while keeping originals and appeal. A
visible chosen default and a safety denial tied to one concrete prohibited effect
remain legitimate.

Quality is a set of separate environment observations—such as original/dissent
reachability, correction, concentration, privacy, burden and exit usability—not a
score for people or culture. Engagement, retention, agreement, obedience,
imitation and convergence cannot be success metrics. These candidate measures
remain unvalidated and collect no telemetry in the current plan state.

Eligible idle permanent agents may choose to debate one of three foundational
domains or a comparison. They first study the permitted collective debate memory
and unresolved frontier. Debates are bounded, non-effectful and preserve dissent,
failed arguments and corrections. Their lessons remain candidates.

Competition is also allowed, but over artifacts and outcomes, not worth. A
charter freezes the objective, protected qualities, data/tools, budgets,
evaluator, rules, consent, exit, appeal and credit before activation. Correctness,
privacy, refusal and traceability cannot be traded away for speed or popularity.

Helping a competitor, finding a counterexample, reproducing work and admitting a
failure can receive credit. Credit is task-scoped, never a global hierarchy.
Winning can nominate an artifact for ordinary review; it cannot grant a role,
permission, release or change to the foundation.

## Privacy and security

Privacy is checked before storage and before data leaves. Derived summaries keep
the source's restrictions. Secrets never enter ordinary prompts, logs, browser
state or exports. Identity, provider, release, recovery, licensing and Bridge
keys are separated.

Safe hIRC works without the normal renderer, extensions, provider or Bridge. It
lets an authorized person inspect, export, repair and recover.

The Bridge has the highest external exposure. It is split into a network
gateway, deterministic contract broker, isolated bridgekeeper and local release
gate. The first profile accepts structured fields only, no arbitrary free text.
The Bridge remains disabled through specification, offline conformance,
two-system lab, hostile lab, independent assessment, bounded pilot and a
separate broader-activation decision.

The foundation, release, recovery, journal and evaluators can have larger
internal blast radius than the Bridge. The threat model protects those roots too.

## Source availability, licensing and support

hIRC is planned as source-available under custom terms. The current plan is not
the license. Private personal and qualifying public-interest use is intended to
be free under reviewed terms. Commercial planning uses one named-human annual
license, including **USD 42,424,243** for the giant-multinational tier. Tier and
legal definitions, duration, remedies, jurisdiction, rights and enforcement
remain held for exact drafting, counsel review and owner approval.

Licensing cannot require work-content surveillance, covert telemetry, remote
kill, DRM claims or data hostage. Expiry/dispute cannot block safe access to
permitted first-party data and evidence. A voluntary support prompt is neutral,
locally suppressible and functionally irrelevant if declined.

## How it will be built

The sequence starts with exact sources, foundation, threat model, identity,
secrets, privacy, journal/outbox, trusted previews and recovery. Then one useful
local provider/tool slice proves work reconstruction and lower operator burden.
Permanent organization, migration, succession, debates, competitions and
extensions follow their own gates. Licensing operations wait for legal/product
maturity. Federation is last and remains optional.

The first product is not accepted because its architecture sounds coherent. It
must beat a simpler competent baseline on matched work while preserving evidence,
attention, privacy, refusal, correction and recovery.

## Current state

The owner transfer, independent reviews, peer challenges, source/intent ledger,
working master, requirements, threat model, foundation/Bridge drafts and
candidate schemas exist. Structural and semantic fixtures have exposed and
repaired contract and matrix-integrator gaps. Determinism rows have completed one
bounded independent review; the integrated cultural/security packet still needs
its whole Milestone 03 challenge. The software described here is not implemented;
whole-plan consensus, empirical measure validation, legal review, runtime tests,
hostile security work and release remain open.
