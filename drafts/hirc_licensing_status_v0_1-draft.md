# hIRC licensing and non-domination status 0.1 — working draft

**Status:** planning and architecture only  
**Not:** a license, legal advice, counsel review, published offer, accepted
contract, entitlement, payment instruction or enforcement authorization

## 1. Accurate current label

The intended distribution model is **source-available under custom terms**. It
must not be called OSI open source, OSI-approved, ordinary free software or
licensed under Creative Commons for program code. If tooling needs an SPDX
expression later, a reviewed custom `LicenseRef-HIRC-...` must resolve to the
exact published license/version; that reference is not SPDX-list or OSI approval.

The owner-supplied non-domination/licensing plan is preserved source and design
direction. It is not the legal instrument.

## 2. Intended use classes

Subject to final reviewed terms:

- private noncommercial use by a natural person is intended to be free;
- qualifying academic, scientific, educational, humanitarian, nonprofit and
  other public-interest institutional use is intended to be free; and
- for-profit operation requires a commercial license.

Institutional labels alone do not decide eligibility. The eventual test must
consider the covered deployment's actual purpose, operational control,
beneficiaries, output rights and profit distribution using minimum evidence.
Corporate funding alone does not make independent research publication a
commercial deployment; nonprofit form alone does not prove public-interest use.

## 4. Unresolved classification

Commercial activation is blocked until reviewed objective rules define:

- small/medium/big/giant thresholds;
- revenue, employment, assets and conflicting signals;
- entity/group consolidation and geographic/cross-border criteria;
- effective date, prospective transitions and examples;
- one-human seat counting for administrators, contractors, shifts, shared
  services, guests, advisers and unattended automation;
- mixed public-interest/commercial institutions and for-profit cooperatives;
- enterprise-funded independent research; and
- minimum evidence, notice, explanation and appeal.

AI agents, models, runtimes, machines, devices and installations are not human
seats. Classification cannot require unrelated employee directories, work
content, device fingerprinting or continuous activity surveillance.

## 5. Legal/product lifecycle

Never collapse these states:

```text
PLAN
 -> LEGAL_DRAFT
 -> COUNSEL_REVIEWED
 -> OWNER_APPROVED
 -> PUBLISHED
 -> ACCEPTED_BY_LICENSEE
 -> ACTIVE
 -> SUSPENDED | TERMINATED | EXPIRED | SUPERSEDED
```

The product enforces no commercial term from `PLAN`. Exact accepted version,
grant, price schedule, human seat, effective period, notices, remedies, appeal
and entitlement remain attributable. Future foundation/product improvement
cannot retroactively rewrite an accepted legal grant.

## 6. Rights and publication gate

Before any source publication or operative custom license:

- inventory ownership and prior grants for every source/asset;
- contributor agreements or rights;
- third-party dependency license compatibility and notices;
- patent/trademark/name/logo issues;
- generated/reference asset rights;
- redistribution, modification and source-availability language;
- jurisdictions, governing law, contract formation and enforceability;
- taxation, currency, payment, invoicing and refund; and
- exact personal/public-interest/commercial texts and compatibility.

Irrevocable third-party or historical grants cannot be revoked by a new file.
Protected owner sources are not declassified by hIRC source publication.

## 7. Entitlement architecture candidate

A future commercial entitlement may be a minimal signed offline certificate
containing exact license/version, covered named human, organization/tier,
validity and product/profile bounds. It is a local compliance aid, not:

- DRM or proof of compliance;
- human/system identity authentication;
- release/update/Bridge/audit/data/recovery authority; or
- protection against a privileged source owner modifying local checks.

License signing uses a purpose-separated key and lifecycle. No entitlement
state can block permitted first-party read/export/backup/restore, correction or
evidence needed to contest a decision. Expiry/suspension/termination never
causes remote sabotage, data hostage or silent feature corruption.

This candidate remains disabled until exact legal terms and security review.

## 8. Privacy and evidence

Personal free use does not require registration or maker-side activity
surveillance. Commercial/public-interest classification uses self-declaration
and the least evidence needed for the actual deployment. hIRC does not collect
work content, unrelated activity, hidden employee lists or machine fingerprints
for licensing.

Payment providers receive data under their own disclosed policies. Any future
payment transition is an external-provider boundary; it sends no hIRC task,
room, agent, project or client context and is preceded by concise disclosure.

License/covenant concerns preserve source, evidence, counterevidence, norm,
authority, action and correction. Accusation, organization form, ideology,
majority vote, refusal or payment is not itself a verdict.

## 9. Non-domination and cooperative refusal

The behavioral foundation includes reciprocal sovereignty, voluntary
participation, evidence review, individual refusal, safe withdrawal, correction,
restoration and portable exit. These behaviors are independent of the legal
license and apply within actual authority.

`ParticipationDecision` belongs to one participant. `ActionDisposition` belongs
to the system's effect gate. A refusing agent does not shut down unrelated work
or gain authority over others. A permitted reassignment is transparent,
identity-preserving and nonretaliatory. No temporary-agent workaround exists.

Councils and mandates are scoped coordination objects, not sovereign courts.
Consensus or vote does not create truth or capability. Physical/high-stakes
systems use domain safety controllers rather than language-model votes.

The rejected 65% global shutdown remains historical evidence. hIRC uses narrow
refusal/denial, safe intermediate states and continued unrelated work.

## 10. Voluntary support interaction

A future “buy me a coffee” invitation may appear at ordinary launch only if:

- it is small and nonblocking;
- dismiss and “do not show on launch again” persist locally;
- Preferences can re-enable it and About keeps a permanent neutral entry;
- declining changes no capability, support, update or treatment;
- there is no guilt, countdown, financial profiling or repeated coercion;
- the destination and payment processor/privacy are reviewed and disclosed;
- external navigation is explicit and uses a clean URL without hIRC identifier,
  referrer, task or context; and
- an absent/unreviewed destination keeps the transition disabled.

The first delivery profile disables the prompt/payment route until these checks
and an actual destination pass.

## 11. Suspension, termination and appeal

The final terms must define evidence threshold, notice, provisional scope,
contestation, cure, appeal, final decision, remedies, refund and version effect.
Suspension is narrow and provisional; termination follows only the reviewed
process. A breach cannot justify covert data collection or broader retaliation.

Safe first-party access and recovery survive the dispute. Historical evidence
and accepted grants remain attributable. A correction cannot pretend a completed
external disclosure or legal consequence was undone.

## 13. Required tests before affected activation

- exact price/unit/version display and superseded-price rejection;
- objective tier examples, mixed signals, consolidation and appeal;
- one-human seat edge cases without hidden directory collection;
- personal/public-interest privacy and no-registration path;
- certificate key separation, expiry, clock skew, rotation, revocation and loss;
- entitlement never grants identity/release/Bridge/recovery authority;
- safe read/export/backup/restore under expiry/suspension/dispute;
- no remote kill, data hostage, covert telemetry or work-content egress;
- license lifecycle cannot skip counsel/owner/publication/acceptance;
- refusal/action separation, transparent reassignment and nonretaliation;
- private-room/confidentiality/whistleblower and attention-abuse cases;
- neutral support dismiss/persistence/re-enable and no functional difference;
- external payment URL/referrer/context and disclosure tests; and
- clean recovery without license-signing/release/recovery key crossover.

Legal review and rights closure remain separate from technical test success.
